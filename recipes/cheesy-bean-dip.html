Thomas's Cheesy Bean Dip

Warm refried beans baked with cream cheese and salsa under a blanket of melted cheddar. Scoop city.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook20 minutes
Serves8 people
Nutritionabout 220 cal · 9g protein per serving

Serve it right in the baking dish — it stays warm longer that way.

What you need

  • 1 can refried beans (one 16-ounce can)
  • 1 block cream cheese, softened (8 ounces)
  • 1 cup salsa
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese, divided (one 8-ounce bag)
  • Tortilla chips (for scooping)

Also grab: a square baking dish and a mixing bowl.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 350 degrees.
  2. Mix it up. Stir the beans, cream cheese, salsa, and 1 cup of the cheddar in a bowl until mostly smooth.
  3. Fill the dish. Spread in a square baking dish and top with the rest of the cheddar.
  4. Bake. Bake 20 minutes until hot and bubbly around the edges.
  5. Serve. Scoop it up hot with tortilla chips.
