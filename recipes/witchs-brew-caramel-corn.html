Thomas's Witch's Brew Caramel Corn

Buttery caramel popcorn tossed with candy corn and a chocolate drizzle. Sweet, salty, and dangerously poppable.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook15 minutes
Serves12 cups
Nutritionabout 180 cal · 1g protein per cup

Stir the popcorn while the caramel's hot — once it cools, it won't budge.

What you need

  • 10 cups popped popcorn (about 1/2 cup unpopped kernels)
  • 1/2 cup butter (one stick)
  • 1 cup brown sugar, packed
  • 1/4 cup corn syrup
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1 cup candy corn
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips, melted (for drizzling)

Also grab: a huge bowl, a saucepan, and a baking sheet with parchment.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 250°F. Put the popcorn in your biggest bowl and line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Make the caramel. Melt the butter in a saucepan, stir in the brown sugar and corn syrup, and boil 4 minutes without stirring. Take it off the heat and stir in the baking soda and vanilla — it'll foam up.
  3. Coat the popcorn. Pour the caramel over the popcorn and stir fast until every piece is coated.
  4. Bake it crisp. Spread on the baking sheet and bake 45 minutes, stirring every 15. (Yes, really — that's what makes it crunchy.)
  5. Finish it. Let it cool, toss with candy corn, and drizzle with melted chocolate.
