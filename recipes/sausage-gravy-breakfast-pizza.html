Thomas's Sausage Gravy Breakfast Pizza

Pizza crust piled with creamy sausage gravy, fluffy scrambled eggs and melted cheddar. Breakfast and pizza had a delicious baby.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep20 minutes
Bake15–18 minutes
Serves6–8 slices
Nutritionabout 620 cal · 28g protein per slice

Let it rest 5 minutes before slicing so the toppings settle.

What you need

  • 1 pound pizza dough (one store ball or tube)
  • 1 pound breakfast sausage (one store package)
  • ¼ cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups milk
  • 8 large eggs
  • 1½ cups shredded cheddar cheese
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons butter

Also grab: a large pizza pan or baking sheet, a large skillet and a whisk.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven to 450°F.. Lightly grease your pizza pan.
  2. Brown the sausage.. Cook the sausage in the skillet over medium-high heat for 8–10 minutes, breaking it into crumbles, until no pink remains. Leave about 2 tablespoons of grease in the pan and drain the rest.
  3. Make the gravy.. Stir the flour into the sausage and cook 1 minute. Slowly pour in the milk while stirring. Keep stirring for about 5 minutes until thick like gravy. Season with the salt and pepper and set aside.
  4. Scramble the eggs.. In the same skillet, melt the butter and softly scramble the eggs for 2–3 minutes. They should still look a little wet — they finish in the oven.
  5. Build the pizza.. Stretch the dough out on the pan. Spread the sausage gravy edge to edge, then the scrambled eggs, then the cheddar.
  6. Bake 15–18 minutes.. It is done when the crust is deep golden and the cheese is bubbly.
  7. Rest and slice.. Wait 5 minutes, then cut into slices and serve hot.

How to serve it

A dash of hot sauce on top is fantastic. Serve with fresh fruit to round it out.

Save the leftovers

Refrigerate for up to 3 days. Reheat slices in a 350°F oven until hot.
