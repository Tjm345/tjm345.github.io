Thomas's Pumpkin Fudge

Creamy, melt-in-your-mouth fudge that tastes like pumpkin pie. No candy thermometer needed.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook5 minutes + chilling
Serves36 squares
Nutritionabout 110 cal · 1g protein per square

Line the pan with parchment hanging over the sides — the fudge lifts right out.

What you need

  • 3 cups white chocolate chips
  • 1 can (14 ounces) sweetened condensed milk
  • 1/2 cup pumpkin puree (plain pumpkin, not pie filling)
  • 1 teaspoon pumpkin pie spice
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt

Also grab: an 8x8 pan, parchment paper, and a medium saucepan.

How to make it

  1. Prep the pan. Line an 8x8 pan with parchment, leaving extra hanging over two sides.
  2. Melt it together. In a saucepan over low heat, stir the white chocolate chips and condensed milk until smooth. Don't walk away — white chocolate burns fast.
  3. Add the pumpkin. Stir in the pumpkin puree, pumpkin pie spice, vanilla, and salt until smooth and orange.
  4. Chill. Spread into the pan and refrigerate at least 3 hours until firm.
  5. Cut. Lift out by the parchment and cut into small squares with a sharp knife.
