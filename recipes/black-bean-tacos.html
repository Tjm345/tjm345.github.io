Thomas's Black Bean Tacos

Warm, filling tacos with seasoned black beans, crunchy cabbage, and cool avocado. Fast, cheap, and really good.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Cook10 minutes
Serves8 tacos
Nutritionabout 310 cal · 11g protein per 2 tacos

Warm the tortillas first — they bend without breaking.

What you need

  • 2 cans black beans (15 ounces each — one regular store can — drained and rinsed)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin (a warm, earthy spice — find it with the other spices)
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 8 small corn tortillas
  • 1 cup shredded cabbage (a small handful per taco)
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges

Also grab: a big pan, a can opener, and a cutting board.

How to make it

  1. Warm the beans. Pour the olive oil into a big pan over medium heat — that is the middle setting on the stove. Add the beans, chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, and salt. Stir and cook for about 5 minutes until the beans are hot all the way through. Mash a few beans with the spoon as you stir — it makes them thick and creamy.
  2. Warm the tortillas. Heat a dry pan with nothing in it over medium heat. Warm each tortilla for about 30 seconds on each side until it bends easily. Stack them on a plate and cover with a clean towel so they stay warm.
  3. Get the toppings ready. Shred the cabbage, dice the tomato, slice the avocado, and cut the lime into wedges.
  4. Build your tacos. Spoon beans into each tortilla, then add cabbage, tomato, and avocado on top. Squeeze lime over everything.
  5. Eat them right away. Tacos are best warm. Leftover beans keep in the fridge for 3 days — just warm them up in a pan.
