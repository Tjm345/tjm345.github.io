Recipe 44 · Lunch

Thomas's Sloppy Joes

Sweet, tangy, saucy ground beef piled high on toasted buns. Messy in the best way — grab extra napkins.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook20 minutes
Serves6 sandwiches
Nutritionabout 450 cal · 26g protein per sandwich

Let the sauce simmer thick so it stays on the bun and not on your shirt.

What you need

  • 2 pounds ground beef (one store package)
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced (or 1 teaspoon garlic powder)
  • 1 cup ketchup
  • ¼ cup brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons yellow mustard
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
  • ½ cup water
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • 6 hamburger buns, toasted

Also grab: a large skillet with a lid, a wooden spoon and a baking sheet for toasting buns.

How to make it

  1. Brown the beef.. Cook the ground beef in the large skillet over medium-high heat for 8–10 minutes, breaking it up with your spoon, until no pink is left. Drain off the grease.
  2. Cook the veggies.. Add the onion and bell pepper to the beef and cook about 5 minutes until they soften up. Stir in the garlic and cook 1 more minute.
  3. Make it saucy.. Stir in the ketchup, brown sugar, mustard, Worcestershire sauce, water, salt and pepper. Mix it all together really well.
  4. Simmer it down.. Turn the heat to low, put the lid on and let it simmer 10 minutes. Take the lid off for the last few minutes so the sauce gets nice and thick — it should cling to the meat, not run off.
  5. Toast the buns.. While the sauce simmers, split the buns and toast them cut-side up on the baking sheet under the broiler for 1–2 minutes, until golden. Watch them close, they burn fast.
  6. Pile it high.. Spoon a big heap of the beef mixture onto each bottom bun, cap it with the top bun, and serve hot with a stack of napkins.

How to serve it

Classic with potato chips and pickles. A slice of cheese on top melts right into the sauce if you want to get fancy.

Save the leftovers

Refrigerate the meat mixture for up to 4 days. It is even better the next day — reheat in a skillet and pile onto fresh toasted buns.
