Thomas's Pigs in a Blanket

Little hot dogs wrapped in soft crescent dough and baked golden brown. The easiest game day snack there is.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook15 minutes
Serves24 pieces
Nutritionabout 90 cal · 3g protein per piece

Don't overfill the pan — leave a little space so the dough can puff up.

What you need

  • 2 cans crescent roll dough (the refrigerated kind — 8-count cans)
  • 1 package mini cocktail hot dogs (like Lit'l Smokies — one regular package)
  • Ketchup and mustard (for dipping)

Also grab: a baking sheet and parchment paper.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 375 degrees and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Cut the dough. Unroll both cans of dough and cut each triangle into 3 smaller strips.
  3. Wrap them up. Roll one little hot dog up in each strip of dough, starting at the wide end.
  4. Bake. Bake 12 to 15 minutes until the dough is puffy and golden brown.
  5. Serve. Let them cool a couple minutes, then put out ketchup and mustard for dipping.
