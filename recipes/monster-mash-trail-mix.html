Thomas's Monster Mash Trail Mix

Candy corn, pretzels, chocolate, and peanuts all mashed together. The bowl empties itself at parties.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
CookNo-bake
Serves10 cups
Nutritionabout 200 cal · 4g protein per cup

Make a double batch — this disappears faster than you think.

What you need

  • 2 cups candy corn
  • 2 cups mini pretzels
  • 2 cups salted peanuts
  • 1 cup chocolate chips or chocolate candies
  • 1 cup candy-coated chocolates (like M&M's — Halloween colors)

Also grab: one giant bowl and a big spoon. That's it.

How to make it

  1. Dump it all in. Pour everything into the biggest bowl you own.
  2. Mix it up. Stir until the colors are evenly mixed — you want candy in every handful.
  3. Serve. Pour into a serving bowl or portion into little bags for handing out.
