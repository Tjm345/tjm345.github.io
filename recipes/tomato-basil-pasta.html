Thomas's Tomato Basil Pasta

Spaghetti in a fresh tomato sauce with lots of basil. Simple, fast, and tastes like summer.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook20 minutes
Serves4 servings
Nutritionabout 420 cal · 12g protein per serving

Save a cup of the pasta water — it makes the sauce silky.

What you need

  • 12 ounces spaghetti (one regular box is usually 16 ounces — use about three-quarters of it)
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced (chop it up small)
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes (28 ounces — one big store can)
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon sugar (just a little to cut the sourness)
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • ½ cup fresh basil leaves (torn into small pieces)
  • 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar (optional — makes the sauce richer)

Also grab: a big pot, a big pan, a colander, and a wooden spoon.

How to make it

  1. Start the pasta water. Fill a big pot with water and a big pinch of salt. Put it on high heat and bring it to a rolling boil — big bubbles all over.
  2. Cook the spaghetti. Add the spaghetti and stir so it does not stick. Cook for 9 to 11 minutes until it is tender but still has a little bite. Before you drain it, scoop out a mug of the starchy pasta water and set it aside.
  3. Start the sauce. While the pasta cooks, heat the olive oil in a big pan over medium heat. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds — just until it smells good, not brown.
  4. Add the tomatoes. Pour in the crushed tomatoes, salt, pepper, and sugar. Stir and let it bubble gently for 10 minutes. Stir in the cherry tomatoes for the last 2 minutes.
  5. Put it together. Drain the pasta and add it straight into the pan with the sauce. Toss it all together. If the sauce is too thick, add a splash of the pasta water you saved until it looks silky.
  6. Finish with basil. Turn off the heat and stir in the basil and the balsamic vinegar if you are using it. Serve right away in bowls.
