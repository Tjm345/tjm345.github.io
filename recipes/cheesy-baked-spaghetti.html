Thomas's Cheesy Baked Spaghetti

Spaghetti tossed in meaty tomato sauce and baked under a blanket of melted cheese. A big, easy dinner for the whole table.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Cook35 minutes
TotalAbout 50 minutes
Serves6–8 people
Nutritionabout 600 cal · 30g protein per serving

Let it rest for 5 minutes so the cheesy servings hold together.

What you need

  • 1 pound spaghetti (one standard box)
  • 1 pound ground beef (one standard package)
  • 1 jar marinara sauce (24 ounces)
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Also grab: a large pot, a large skillet and a 9 × 13-inch baking dish.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F. Cook the spaghetti in salted water until it is just tender, then drain it.
  2. Brown the beef. Cook it in a large skillet over medium heat for 8–10 minutes, breaking it into crumbles. Drain the grease.
  3. Make the sauce. Stir in the marinara, garlic powder, Italian seasoning, salt and pepper. Let it simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. Put it together. Toss the spaghetti with the meat sauce, then spread everything in the baking dish.
  5. Add the cheese. Sprinkle the mozzarella and Parmesan evenly over the top.
  6. Bake for 20 minutes. It is ready when the sauce is bubbling and the cheese has golden spots.
  7. Let it rest for 5 minutes before serving.

Make it a meal

Serve it with garlic bread for an easy dinner that feeds a crowd.

Save the leftovers

Cover and refrigerate for up to 4 days. This one reheats great.
