Thomas's Candy Corn Parfait

Layers of orange cream, whipped topping, and candy corn in a glass — looks just like a giant candy corn.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
CookNo-bake
Serves4 parfaits
Nutritionabout 280 cal · 3g protein per parfait

Chill the glasses first — the layers stay prettier in a cold glass.

What you need

  • 1 box (3.4 ounces) vanilla instant pudding mix (one small box)
  • 1 1/2 cups cold milk
  • Orange food coloring (a few drops)
  • 1 tub (8 ounces) whipped topping, thawed
  • 1 cup candy corn

Also grab: 4 clear glasses, a big bowl, and an electric mixer or whisk.

How to make it

  1. Make it orange. Whisk the pudding mix and milk for 2 minutes, then stir in a few drops of orange food coloring until it looks like a pumpkin.
  2. Layer it up. In each glass, spoon in orange pudding, then a layer of whipped topping, then a sprinkle of candy corn. Repeat once.
  3. Top it off. Finish with a swirl of whipped topping and a few candy corn pieces standing up on top.
  4. Chill. Refrigerate 30 minutes so the layers set, then serve with a spoon.
