Thomas's Taco Rice Bowls

Seasoned beef, rice, and all the toppings in a bowl. Taco night, no shell needed.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook15 minutes
Serves4 servings
Nutritionabout 520 cal · 30g protein per serving

Set the toppings out and let everyone build their own bowl.

What you need

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 packet taco seasoning (or 2 tablespoons)
  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • Tortilla chips for crunch, optional

Also grab: a pan and a spoon.

How to make it

  1. Brown the beef. Cook the beef in a pan till browned, then drain.
  2. Season it. Stir in the taco seasoning and a splash of water, and cook 2 more minutes.
  3. Build the bowls. Spoon rice into bowls, top with beef, cheese, tomato, and sour cream.
  4. Serve. Add chips on top if you like, and serve warm.
