Thomas's Sausage Tortellini Soup

Cheesy tortellini, savory sausage, and tomatoes in a warm broth. Cozy soup that comes together fast.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook25 minutes
Serves6 bowls
Nutritionabout 380 cal · 18g protein per bowl

Stir the tortellini in last so it doesn't get mushy.

What you need

  • 1 pound Italian sausage (mild or hot, casings removed if links)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced (or 1 teaspoon garlic powder)
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes (28 ounces)
  • 4 cups chicken broth (one big box)
  • 1 bag cheese tortellini (about 20 ounces, fresh or frozen)
  • 2 cups baby spinach (two big handfuls)
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • Grated Parmesan for the top (optional)

Also grab: a big pot and a spoon.

How to make it

  1. Cook the sausage. In a big pot, cook the sausage and onion, breaking up the sausage, till browned.
  2. Add garlic and tomatoes. Stir in the garlic, then the crushed tomatoes, broth, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper.
  3. Simmer. Bring to a boil, then turn down and simmer 15 minutes.
  4. Add the tortellini. Stir in the tortellini and cook as long as the bag says, usually 5 to 7 minutes.
  5. Add spinach and serve. Stir in the spinach till wilted, top with Parmesan if you like, and serve.
