Thomas's Cinnamon Apple Blondies

Soft, chewy bars with apple pieces and cinnamon. Like apple pie in bar form.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Cook30 minutes
Serves16 bars
Nutritionabout 240 cal · 3g protein per bar

Don't overbake — they firm up as they cool.

What you need

  • 2 apples, peeled and diced small
  • 1/2 cup butter, melted
  • 1 cup brown sugar (packed)
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1 1/2 cups flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt

Also grab: a bowl, a pan (8x8), and a spoon.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 350 degrees.
  2. Mix the wet stuff. In a bowl, mix the melted butter, brown sugar, egg, and vanilla.
  3. Add the dry stuff. Stir in the flour, baking powder, cinnamon, and salt till just mixed.
  4. Fold in the apples. Fold in the diced apples.
  5. Bake. Spread in the pan and bake 30 minutes till golden at the edges and set in the middle.
  6. Cool and cut. Cool completely, then cut into bars.
