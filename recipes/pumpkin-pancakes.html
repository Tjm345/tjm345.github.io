Thomas's Pumpkin Pancakes

Fluffy pancakes with pumpkin and warm spices. Fall breakfast any time of year.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook15 minutes
Serves4 servings
Nutritionabout 320 cal · 7g protein per serving

Flip when bubbles pop on top and the edges look set.

What you need

  • 1 1/2 cups flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon pumpkin pie spice (or more cinnamon)
  • 1 cup pumpkin puree
  • 1 cup milk (whole or 2%)
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons butter, melted
  • Syrup for serving

Also grab: a bowl, a whisk, and a pan.

How to make it

  1. Mix the dry stuff. In a bowl, mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon, and pumpkin pie spice.
  2. Mix the wet stuff. In another bowl, mix the pumpkin, milk, egg, and melted butter.
  3. Combine. Pour the wet into the dry and stir till just mixed — lumps are fine.
  4. Cook. Spoon onto a buttered pan over medium heat, cook till bubbles pop, flip, and cook 1 more minute.
  5. Serve. Serve warm with syrup.
