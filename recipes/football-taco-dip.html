Thomas's Football Taco Dip

Layers of beans, seasoned sour cream, guacamole, and cheese in one dish. Every scoop gets a little of everything.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep20 minutes
CookNo-bake
Serves10 people
Nutritionabout 260 cal · 8g protein per serving

Chill it at least 30 minutes — the layers slice cleaner and taste better cold.

What you need

  • 1 can refried beans (one 16-ounce can)
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 1 cup guacamole (one store tub)
  • 1 cup salsa
  • 2 cups shredded Mexican-blend cheese (one 8-ounce bag)
  • Diced tomatoes and sliced black olives (for the top)
  • Tortilla chips (for scooping)

Also grab: a 9x13 dish and a spatula.

How to make it

  1. Bean layer. Spread the refried beans flat in the bottom of the dish.
  2. Sour cream layer. Stir the taco seasoning into the sour cream and spread it over the beans.
  3. Guac and salsa. Spread the guacamole over that, then spoon the salsa on top.
  4. Cheese and toppings. Cover with shredded cheese, then scatter tomatoes and olives over it.
  5. Chill and serve. Refrigerate 30 minutes, then serve cold with tortilla chips.
