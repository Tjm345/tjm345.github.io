Thomas's Pulled Pork Sandwiches

Pork shoulder slow-cooked all day until it falls apart, then piled on buns with BBQ sauce and coleslaw.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Cook8 hours
Serves10 sandwiches
Nutritionabout 520 cal · 32g protein per sandwich

Don't lift the lid while it cooks — every peek adds cook time.

What you need

  • 1 pork shoulder roast (about 4 pounds — also called pork butt)
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon paprika
  • 2 teaspoons garlic powder
  • 2 teaspoons salt
  • 1 cup BBQ sauce, plus more for serving
  • 10 hamburger buns
  • Coleslaw (one store tub — optional but great on top)

Also grab: a crockpot, two forks, and a cutting board.

How to make it

  1. Season the pork. Rub the brown sugar, paprika, garlic powder, and salt all over the pork roast.
  2. Slow cook. Put it in the crockpot, cover, and cook on low 8 hours until it shreds easily.
  3. Shred it. Move the pork to a cutting board and pull it apart with two forks. Toss the bone and any big fat pieces.
  4. Sauce it. Put the shredded pork back in the crockpot and stir in the BBQ sauce.
  5. Build sandwiches. Pile pork on buns, add more BBQ sauce and a scoop of coleslaw if you want.
