Thomas's Loaded Baked Potato Soup

All the best parts of a loaded baked potato in soup form — bacon, cheddar, sour cream, the works.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Cook25 minutes
Serves6 people
Nutritionabout 600 cal · 20g protein per serving

Leave the potato a little chunky for a thick, hearty bowl.

What you need

  • 4 big russet potatoes, baked and cooled
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • ¼ cup all-purpose flour
  • 4 cups milk
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • 1½ cups shredded cheddar cheese
  • ½ cup sour cream
  • 6 slices bacon, cooked crisp and crumbled
  • Sliced green onions
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon pepper

Also grab: a big pot, a whisk, a spoon and a potato masher or fork.

How to make it

  1. Scoop out the potatoes. Put the insides of the baked potatoes in a bowl and roughly mash them. Chunky is good.
  2. Start the soup. Melt the butter in a big pot over medium heat. Whisk in the flour and cook for 1 minute, stirring.
  3. Add the liquid. Slowly whisk in the milk and broth. Simmer, stirring, until thickened, about 8–10 minutes.
  4. Add the potato and cheese. Stir in the potato, then add the cheddar a handful at a time until melted.
  5. Finish it. Take the pot off the heat and stir in the sour cream, salt and pepper.
  6. Load the bowls. Top each serving with bacon, green onions and extra cheese.
