Thomas's Cheesy Corn Dip

Warm, creamy corn dip with melted cheese. Perfect with tortilla chips for scooping.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook20 minutes
Serves6 servings
Nutritionabout 260 cal · 8g protein per serving

Stir it once halfway through so the cheese melts evenly.

What you need

  • 2 cans corn, drained (or 3 cups frozen corn)
  • 8 ounces cream cheese, softened (one block)
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 can green chiles, drained (small can, optional)
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • Tortilla chips for scooping

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 375 degrees.
  2. Mix it up. In a bowl, mix the corn, cream cheese, cheddar, mayonnaise, green chiles if using, garlic powder, and salt.
  3. Bake. Spread in a baking dish and bake 20 minutes till hot and bubbly.
  4. Serve. Serve warm with tortilla chips.
