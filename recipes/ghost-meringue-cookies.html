Thomas's Ghost Meringue Cookies

Crisp, airy little ghosts with chocolate chip eyes. They melt in your mouth and look adorable on a party table.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep20 minutes
Cook90 minutes
Serves24 ghosts
Nutritionabout 25 cal · 1g protein per ghost

Make sure your bowl is squeaky clean — any grease keeps the whites from whipping up.

What you need

  • 3 egg whites, room temperature
  • 1/4 teaspoon cream of tartar
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla
  • Mini chocolate chips (for eyes)

Also grab: a baking sheet, parchment paper, and an electric mixer (this one's hard by hand).

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 225°F — low and slow. Line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Whip the whites. Beat the egg whites and cream of tartar with a mixer until foamy, then slowly add the sugar and beat until stiff shiny peaks form. Beat in the vanilla.
  3. Pipe the ghosts. Spoon the meringue into a zip-top bag, snip the corner, and pipe little ghost blobs about 2 inches tall.
  4. Add eyes. Press 2 mini chocolate chips into each ghost for eyes.
  5. Bake low and slow. Bake 90 minutes, then turn the oven off and leave them inside 1 hour to dry out. They should lift right off the paper.
