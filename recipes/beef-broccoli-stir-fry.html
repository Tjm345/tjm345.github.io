Thomas's Beef and Broccoli Stir Fry

Tender beef and crisp broccoli in a savory sauce, over rice. Faster than takeout.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Cook15 minutes
Serves4 servings
Nutritionabout 420 cal · 32g protein per serving

Slice the beef thin against the grain so it stays tender.

What you need

  • 1 pound beef strips (sirloin or stir-fry beef)
  • 4 cups broccoli florets (one bag or one head)
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch (mixed with 2 tablespoons water)
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced (chopped very small)
  • 1 tablespoon oil (vegetable or sesame)
  • 2 cups cooked rice (for serving)

Also grab: a big pan or wok, and a spoon.

How to make it

  1. Mix the sauce. In a small bowl, mix the soy sauce, brown sugar, and cornstarch-water mix.
  2. Sear the beef. Heat the oil in a big pan over high heat, add the beef, and cook 2–3 minutes till browned. Take it out.
  3. Cook the broccoli. In the same pan, add the broccoli and a splash of water, cover, and cook 3 minutes till bright green and a little crisp.
  4. Bring it together. Put the beef back, add the garlic and sauce, and stir till the sauce thickens.
  5. Serve. Serve over rice.
