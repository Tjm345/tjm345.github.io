Thomas's Chicken Pesto Panini

Grilled chicken, pesto, and melted cheese pressed in crusty bread. Crispy outside, melty inside.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook10 minutes
Serves2 sandwiches
Nutritionabout 520 cal · 38g protein per sandwich

Press it down while it cooks so the cheese melts all the way.

What you need

  • 4 slices bread (ciabatta or sourdough)
  • 1 cup cooked chicken, sliced or shredded
  • 4 tablespoons pesto (store-bought jar)
  • 4 slices mozzarella cheese
  • 1 tomato, sliced (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons butter (softened)

Also grab: a pan and a spatula (or a panini press if you have one).

How to make it

  1. Build the sandwiches. Spread pesto on 2 slices, layer chicken, mozzarella, and tomato if using. Top with the other slices.
  2. Butter the outside. Spread butter on the outside of each sandwich.
  3. Cook and press. Cook in a pan over medium heat, pressing down with the spatula, 3–4 minutes per side till golden and melty.
  4. Serve. Cut in half and serve warm.
