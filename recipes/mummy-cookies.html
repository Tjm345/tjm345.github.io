Thomas's Mummy Cookies

Peanut butter sandwich cookies wrapped in white chocolate bandages with candy eyes. Almost too cute to eat.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep25 minutes
CookNo-bake
Serves20 cookies
Nutritionabout 160 cal · 2g protein per cookie

Let the bandages be messy — real mummies aren't neat either.

What you need

  • 20 peanut butter sandwich cookies (like Nutter Butters — one package)
  • 2 cups white chocolate chips
  • 1 tablespoon coconut oil or vegetable oil
  • 40 candy eyes

Also grab: a baking sheet, parchment paper, and a microwave-safe bowl.

How to make it

  1. Set up. Line a baking sheet with parchment and lay out the cookies.
  2. Melt the chocolate. Microwave the white chocolate chips with the oil in 20-second bursts, stirring until smooth.
  3. Wrap the mummies. Spoon the white chocolate over each cookie, then drag a fork across to make bandage lines. Leave a little gap near the top.
  4. Add eyes. Press 2 candy eyes into the gap on each cookie before the chocolate sets.
  5. Let them set. Rest 30 minutes until firm, then serve.
