Thomas's Garlic Butter Shrimp Pasta

Juicy shrimp and linguine tossed in garlic butter with lemon. Tastes fancy, takes 25 minutes.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook15 minutes
Serves4 people
Nutritionabout 650 cal · 40g protein per serving

The shrimp are ready when they turn pink and curl into a loose C shape.

What you need

  • 12 ounces linguine (one box)
  • 1 pound large shrimp, peeled and deveined (one bag, thawed if frozen)
  • 6 tablespoons butter
  • 5 cloves garlic, minced
  • ½ teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • ¼ cup chopped fresh parsley
  • Salt and pepper
  • Grated Parmesan, for serving

Also grab: a large pot, a big pan, a colander, tongs and a measuring cup.

How to make it

  1. Cook the linguine. Boil it in salty water until just tender. Save ½ cup of the pasta water, then drain.
  2. Start the garlic butter. While the pasta cooks, melt the butter in a big pan over medium heat. Add the garlic and red pepper flakes, then cook for 1 minute until it smells amazing.
  3. Cook the shrimp. Add them in a single layer, season with salt and pepper, and cook for 2 minutes per side until pink and cooked through.
  4. Toss everything together. Squeeze in the lemon juice, then add the pasta and parsley. Add splashes of pasta water until the sauce coats everything silky.
  5. Serve it hot with Parmesan on top.
