Thomas's Triple-Stack Club Sandwich

Triple-decker with ham, turkey, crispy bacon and melty cheese—the kind of sandwich that needs a toothpick to hold it together. Makes four big club sandwiches.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Cook10 minutes
TotalAbout 25 minutes
Makes4 sandwiches
Nutritionabout 850 cal · 55g protein per sandwich

Use four toothpicks in each sandwich before cutting so every stacked quarter stays together.

What you need

  • 12 slices sandwich bread (white, wheat or sourdough)
  • 8 slices deli ham
  • 8 slices deli turkey
  • 8 strips bacon
  • 4 slices Swiss or cheddar cheese
  • 4 lettuce leaves
  • 1 large tomato, sliced
  • ¼ cup mayo
  • 2 tablespoons mustard
  • Toothpicks

Also grab: a skillet, paper towels, a toaster, a small bowl and a sharp knife.

How to make them

  1. Cook the bacon. Put it in a skillet over medium heat and cook for 8–10 minutes, until crispy. Move it to paper towels to drain.
  2. Toast the bread. Toast all 12 slices until golden.
  3. Mix the spread. Stir the mayo and mustard together, then spread it on one side of every slice of toast.
  4. Build each sandwich. Start with one slice of toast, 2 slices of ham and a slice of cheese. Add a second slice of toast, then 2 slices of turkey, 2 strips of bacon, lettuce and tomato. Finish with the third slice of toast.
  5. Hold it together and cut it. Push 4 toothpicks through each sandwich, one in each quarter. Cut diagonally into 4 triangles.

Best served fresh

Eat these right after you build them. The toast goes soft if the sandwiches sit too long.

Prep ahead

Cook the bacon and slice everything ahead, then assemble when you're ready. Cooked bacon keeps in the fridge for 4–5 days.
