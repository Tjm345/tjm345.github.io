Thomas's Chicken Pot Pie

Creamy chicken and veggies tucked under a golden pie crust. Comfort food at its best.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep20 minutes
Cook40 minutes
Serves6 slices
Nutritionabout 480 cal · 22g protein per slice

Cut a few slits in the top crust so steam can escape — no soggy top.

What you need

  • 1 store rotisserie chicken, shredded (about 3 cups of meat)
  • 1 bag frozen mixed vegetables, thawed (one 12-ounce bag — peas, carrots, corn, green beans)
  • 1 can cream of chicken soup (one 10.5-ounce can)
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 box refrigerated pie crusts (2 crusts in the box)

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 400 degrees.
  2. Mix the filling. Stir the shredded chicken, veggies, soup, and milk together in a big bowl.
  3. Bottom crust. Unroll one pie crust and press it into the pie dish, letting the edges hang over.
  4. Fill and top. Pour in the chicken mixture, then lay the second crust on top. Pinch the edges together and cut 3 slits in the top.
  5. Bake. Bake 35 to 40 minutes until the crust is deep golden. Cool 10 minutes before slicing.
