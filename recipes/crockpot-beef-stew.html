Thomas's Crockpot Beef Stew

Chunks of beef, potatoes, and carrots simmered all day in thick gravy. The house smells amazing by dinner.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep20 minutes
Cook8 hours
Serves6 bowls
Nutritionabout 420 cal · 30g protein per bowl

Cut the veggies big — small pieces turn to mush over 8 hours.

What you need

  • 2 pounds beef stew meat (one store package — already cut up)
  • 4 potatoes, cut in big chunks
  • 4 carrots, peeled and cut in big pieces
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 4 cups beef broth (one 32-ounce box)
  • 1/3 cup flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

Also grab: a crockpot and a cutting board.

How to make it

  1. Load the crockpot. Put the beef, potatoes, carrots, and onion in the crockpot.
  2. Make it thick. Whisk the flour, salt, and pepper into the beef broth and pour it over everything.
  3. Cook low and slow. Cover and cook on low 8 hours until the beef is fork-tender.
  4. Serve. Stir it up and ladle into bowls. The gravy should be nice and thick.
