Thomas's Chili Cheese Fries

Crispy fries piled with hot chili and melted cheddar. Made for eating on the couch during the game.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep5 minutes
Cook25 minutes
Serves4 people
Nutritionabout 520 cal · 18g protein per serving

Broil the last 2 minutes if you want the cheese extra bubbly — but watch it close.

What you need

  • 1 bag frozen french fries (one regular bag)
  • 1 can chili with beans (one regular can — any brand)
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese (one 8-ounce bag)
  • Sour cream and sliced green onions (optional, for the top)

Also grab: a big baking sheet and parchment paper.

How to make it

  1. Bake the fries. Spread the fries on a lined baking sheet and bake per the bag directions until crispy.
  2. Heat the chili. While the fries bake, warm the chili in a pot on the stove, stirring now and then.
  3. Load them up. Pile the hot fries on a platter, spoon the chili all over, then shower with cheddar.
  4. Melt it. Stick the platter back in the oven 3 to 5 minutes until the cheese is melty.
  5. Top and serve. Add sour cream and green onions if you want, then dig in while it's hot.
