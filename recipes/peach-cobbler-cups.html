Thomas's Peach Cobbler Cups

Juicy peaches with a golden biscuit top, baked in little cups. Summer in a cup.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook25 minutes
Serves6 cups
Nutritionabout 260 cal · 3g protein per cup

Canned peaches work great here — drain them first.

What you need

  • 2 cans peach slices, drained (or 4 fresh peaches, sliced)
  • 1/4 cup sugar (for the peaches)
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup sugar (for the topping)
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 cup milk (whole or 2%)
  • 4 tablespoons butter, melted
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon

Also grab: a bowl, a spoon, and 6 ramekins or a muffin tin.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 375 degrees.
  2. Sweeten the peaches. In a bowl, mix the drained peaches with 1/4 cup sugar.
  3. Make the topping. In another bowl, mix the flour, 1/2 cup sugar, baking powder, milk, melted butter, and cinnamon till smooth.
  4. Fill the cups. Spoon peaches into the cups, then spoon topping over.
  5. Bake. Bake 25 minutes till golden and bubbly.
  6. Serve. Serve warm, plain or with a scoop of ice cream if you like.
