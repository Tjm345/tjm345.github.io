Thomas's Deviled Eggs

Creamy, tangy egg halves with a sprinkle of paprika. The party snack that disappears first.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep20 minutes
Cook10 minutes
Serves6 servings
Nutritionabout 90 cal · 5g protein per half

Pipe the filling with a zip bag with the corner snipped for a neat look.

What you need

  • 6 eggs
  • 3 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 1 teaspoon mustard
  • 1 teaspoon vinegar (white)
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • Paprika for sprinkling

Also grab: a pot, a bowl, and a fork.

How to make it

  1. Boil the eggs. Put the eggs in a pot, cover with water, boil, then cover and sit 10 minutes. Cool and peel.
  2. Halve them. Cut each egg in half and pop the yolks into a bowl.
  3. Make the filling. Mash the yolks with mayonnaise, mustard, vinegar, and salt till smooth.
  4. Fill and sprinkle. Spoon the filling back into the whites and sprinkle with paprika.
  5. Serve. Serve cold.
