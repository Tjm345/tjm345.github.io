Thomas's Bacon, Egg & Cheese Breakfast Sandwiches

Crispy bacon, a fried egg and melty cheese on a toasted English muffin. The drive-thru has nothing on these.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook15 minutes
Serves4 sandwiches
Nutritionabout 520 cal · 26g protein per sandwich

Let the cheese sit on the hot egg for a minute — it melts perfectly on its own.

What you need

  • 8 slices bacon (about half a store package)
  • 4 large eggs
  • 4 English muffins (one store package)
  • 4 slices cheddar cheese
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • salt and black pepper

How to make it

  1. Cook the bacon.. Lay the bacon in a cold skillet, then turn the heat to medium. Cook 8–10 minutes, flipping once, until crispy. Move to a paper-towel-lined plate.
  2. Fry the eggs.. Pour out most of the bacon grease, leaving a thin coating. Crack in the eggs, season with salt and pepper, and fry 3–4 minutes until the whites are set. Pop the yolks if you don't want them runny.
  3. Toast the muffins.. While the eggs cook, split the English muffins and toast them until golden.
  4. Build the sandwiches.. Butter the toasted muffins. On each bottom half, stack 2 slices of bacon, one fried egg and a slice of cheddar. The heat from the egg melts the cheese.
  5. Cap and serve.. Put the tops on and serve right away while everything is hot.

How to serve it

Great on their own, or with a side of fresh fruit. Add hot sauce if you like a kick.

Save the leftovers

Wrap tightly and refrigerate for up to 2 days. Reheat in a 350°F oven for 10 minutes.
