Thomas's Avocado Toast

Creamy avocado on crunchy toast with a little salt and spice. Simple, fresh, and filling.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook5 minutes
Serves2 servings
Nutritionabout 280 cal · 6g protein per serving

Mash the avocado right before you toast the bread so it doesn't turn brown.

What you need

  • 2 slices bread (thick, sourdough or whole wheat)
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 1/2 teaspoon lemon juice
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)
  • 1 egg (optional, fried or boiled)

Also grab: a toaster, a bowl, and a fork.

How to make it

  1. Toast the bread. Toast the bread till golden and crunchy.
  2. Mash the avocado. In a bowl, mash the avocado with lemon juice and salt.
  3. Top the toast. Spread the avocado thick on each slice.
  4. Finish it. Sprinkle with red pepper flakes, add an egg on top if you like, and serve right away.
