Thomas's Ranch Pretzel Bites

Crunchy mini pretzels baked in buttery ranch seasoning. Dangerously easy to keep eating by the handful.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep5 minutes
Cook20 minutes
Serves10 people
Nutritionabout 180 cal · 3g protein per serving

Stir the pretzels halfway through baking so the seasoning coats evenly.

What you need

  • 1 bag mini pretzel twists (one 16-ounce bag)
  • 1/2 cup butter, melted (one stick)
  • 1 packet ranch seasoning mix
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder

Also grab: a big baking sheet and parchment paper.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 300 degrees and line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Coat the pretzels. Dump the pretzels in a big bowl, pour the melted butter over, and toss. Sprinkle the ranch mix and garlic powder over and toss again.
  3. Bake low and slow. Spread on the sheet and bake 20 minutes, stirring once halfway.
  4. Cool. Let them cool on the pan — they get crunchier as they cool. Store in a zip-top bag.
