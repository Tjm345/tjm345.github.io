Thomas's Buffalo Chicken Wraps

Spicy buffalo chicken, cool ranch, and crunchy lettuce wrapped up in a soft tortilla. Fast lunch with a kick.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Cook10 minutes
Serves4 wraps
Nutritionabout 380 cal · 28g protein per wrap

Warm the tortillas first — they fold without cracking.

What you need

  • 1 pound chicken tenders (or sliced chicken breast)
  • 1/2 cup buffalo sauce (one small bottle, like Frank's)
  • 2 tablespoons butter, melted
  • 4 big flour tortillas (the burrito-size kind)
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • 1/2 cup ranch dressing
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 tomato, diced (optional)

Also grab: a pan, a bowl, and a microwave or pan to warm the tortillas.

How to make it

  1. Cook the chicken. Cook the chicken tenders in a pan on medium heat till cooked through, about 4 minutes per side, then slice them up.
  2. Toss in sauce. In a bowl, mix the buffalo sauce and melted butter, then toss the sliced chicken in it.
  3. Warm the tortillas. Warm each tortilla in the microwave for 10 seconds or in a dry pan, so they bend easy.
  4. Build the wraps. Down the middle of each tortilla, layer lettuce, buffalo chicken, cheese, tomato if using, and a drizzle of ranch.
  5. Roll and serve. Fold in the sides, roll it up tight, and cut in half if you like.
