Thomas's BBQ Pulled Chicken Sandwiches

Chicken breasts slow-cooked in BBQ sauce until they shred with a fork. Sweet, smoky, and stupid easy.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook4 hours
Serves8 sandwiches
Nutritionabout 420 cal · 30g protein per sandwich

Two forks, pull in opposite directions — the chicken falls apart on its own.

What you need

  • 2 pounds chicken breasts (about 4 big ones)
  • 1 1/2 cups BBQ sauce
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 8 hamburger buns
  • Pickles and coleslaw (optional, for the top)

Also grab: a crockpot and two forks.

How to make it

  1. Load the crockpot. Lay the chicken breasts in the crockpot.
  2. Sauce it. Stir the BBQ sauce, brown sugar, and Worcestershire together and pour over the chicken.
  3. Cook. Cover and cook on low 4 hours until the chicken shreds easily.
  4. Shred. Pull the chicken apart with two forks right in the pot and stir it into the sauce.
  5. Serve. Pile onto buns and top with pickles or coleslaw if you want.
