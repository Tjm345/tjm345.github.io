Thomas's Banana Nut Oatmeal

Warm oatmeal with sweet banana and crunchy nuts. A cozy breakfast that keeps you full.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep5 minutes
Cook10 minutes
Serves2 bowls
Nutritionabout 320 cal · 8g protein per bowl

Mash half the banana into the oats and slice the rest on top.

What you need

  • 1 cup oats (old-fashioned)
  • 2 cups milk (whole or 2%)
  • 1 banana, sliced
  • 1/4 cup chopped walnuts (or any nuts)
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon

How to make it

  1. Heat the milk. Pour the milk into a pot and heat till it just starts to bubble.
  2. Add the oats. Stir in the oats and turn the heat low.
  3. Cook. Cook 5 minutes, stirring now and then, till thick.
  4. Add banana and cinnamon. Mash half the banana into the oats and stir in the cinnamon.
  5. Serve. Spoon into bowls and top with the rest of the banana, the nuts, and a drizzle of honey.
