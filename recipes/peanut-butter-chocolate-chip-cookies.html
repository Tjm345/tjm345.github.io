Thomas's Peanut Butter Chocolate Chip Cookies

Soft, chewy peanut butter cookies absolutely loaded with chocolate chips. The hardest part is not eating the dough.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Cook12 minutes
Makes24 cookies
Nutritionabout 220 cal · 4g protein per cookie

Pull them out while the centers still look a little soft so they stay chewy.

What you need

  • 1 cup creamy peanut butter
  • ½ cup butter, softened (1 stick)
  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • ½ cup white sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 1½ cups chocolate chips

Also grab: two baking sheets, parchment paper, a mixing bowl, a mixer or sturdy spoon and a cooling rack.

How to make them

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Line baking sheets with parchment.
  2. Beat the butter and sugar. Mix the peanut butter, butter and both sugars until fluffy, about 2 minutes.
  3. Add the wet ingredients. Beat in the eggs and vanilla.
  4. Finish the dough. Stir in the flour, baking soda and salt just until combined, then fold in the chocolate chips.
  5. Scoop the cookies. Put tablespoon-size balls on the sheets about 2 inches apart.
  6. Bake for 10–12 minutes until the edges are set but the centers still look a little soft.
  7. Cool for 5 minutes on the pan, then move the cookies to a rack. Try to save some for other people.
