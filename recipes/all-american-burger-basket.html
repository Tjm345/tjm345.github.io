Thomas's All-American Burger Basket

A diner-style spread with juicy cheeseburgers, crispy seasoned fries and a thick vanilla shake. Makes four servings.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep20 minutes
Cook30 minutes
TotalAbout 50 minutes
Serves4 people
Nutritionabout 1,500 cal · 55g protein per serving (burger + fries + shake)

Make the shake last so it stays cold and thick when the burgers and fries are ready.

What you need

  • For the burgers
  • 1 pound ground beef (one standard package)
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • ½ teaspoon onion powder
  • 4 slices cheddar cheese
  • 4 burger buns
  • Lettuce, tomato, onion, pickles, ketchup, mustard and mayo
  • For the fries
  • 3 large russet potatoes (or one bag of frozen fries)
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon paprika
  • ¼ teaspoon garlic powder
  • For the shake
  • 3 cups vanilla ice cream
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons chocolate syrup (optional)
  • Whipped cream (optional)

Also grab: a baking sheet, a large skillet or grill, a spatula and a blender.

How to make it

  1. Start with the fries—they take the longest. Heat the oven to 425°F. Cut the potatoes into thin strips, then toss them with the oil, salt, paprika and garlic powder. Spread them in one layer on a baking sheet. Bake for 25–30 minutes, flipping once halfway through, until golden and crispy.
  2. Make the patties. Gently mix the salt, pepper, garlic powder and onion powder into the ground beef. Don't overwork it. Form four patties, then press your thumb into the center of each one so they don't puff up.
  3. Cook the burgers. Put the patties in a hot skillet or on the grill for 3–4 minutes per side. Add a slice of cheese during the last minute so it melts. Toast the buns cut-side down in the skillet.
  4. Build the burgers. Add your favorite toppings and sauces to each one.
  5. Make the shake last so it stays thick. Blend the ice cream, milk and chocolate syrup, if you're using it, for about 30 seconds. Pour into tall glasses and add whipped cream if you like.

Best served fresh

Bring the burgers, hot fries and cold shakes to the table as soon as everything is ready.

Save the leftovers

Cooked patties keep in the fridge for 3 days. Reheat fries in the oven or air fryer to crisp them back up—the microwave makes them soggy.
