Thomas's Creamy Sausage & Potato Soup

Hearty Italian sausage, tender potatoes and fresh spinach in a rich, creamy broth. Tastes like fall in a bowl.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Cook30 minutes
Serves6 bowls
Nutritionabout 520 cal · 18g protein per serving

Simmer until the potatoes are completely fork-tender.

What you need

  • 1 pound ground Italian sausage (one store package — mild or hot)
  • 4 big russet potatoes, peeled and cut into small cubes
  • 1 onion, diced (one medium onion)
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced (or 1 teaspoon from a jar)
  • 4 cups chicken broth (one 32-ounce box)
  • 1 cup heavy cream (half of a pint carton)
  • 2 cups fresh spinach (two big handfuls)
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • grated parmesan for serving (optional)

Also grab: a big pot, a spoon, a knife and a cutting board.

How to make it

  1. Brown the sausage. Cook the sausage in a big pot over medium-high heat, breaking it into crumbles with your spoon, for 6–8 minutes until no pink remains. Scoop it out onto a plate, leaving the drippings in the pot.
  2. Soften the onion. Add the onion to the pot and cook 4–5 minutes, stirring, until soft. Stir in the garlic and cook 30 seconds more.
  3. Add potatoes and broth. Stir in the potatoes and pour in the chicken broth. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer 15 minutes, until the potatoes are fork-tender.
  4. Make it creamy. Stir the sausage back in. Pour in the cream, add the spinach, salt and pepper, and simmer 3–4 minutes until the spinach wilts.
  5. Serve it up. Ladle into bowls and top with parmesan if you like. Crusty bread on the side is never a bad idea.
