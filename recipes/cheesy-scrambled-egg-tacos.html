Thomas's Cheesy Scrambled Egg Tacos

Soft scrambled eggs, crispy sausage and melty cheddar tucked into warm tortillas. Breakfast tacos beat breakfast plates.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep5 minutes
Cook10 minutes
Serves4 people (8 tacos)
Nutritionabout 550 cal · 28g protein per serving (2 tacos)

Take the eggs off the heat while they still look a little shiny so they stay soft.

What you need

  • 8 small flour tortillas
  • 8 large eggs
  • ¼ cup milk
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • ½ pound breakfast sausage (one small package)
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • Salsa and sour cream, for serving

Also grab: a big pan, a mixing bowl, a whisk and a spatula.

How to make them

  1. Cook the sausage. Brown it in a big pan over medium-high heat, breaking it into crumbles, about 5–6 minutes. Scoop it out onto a plate.
  2. Whisk the eggs. Mix the eggs, milk and a pinch of salt and pepper in a bowl.
  3. Start the eggs. Melt the butter in the same pan over medium-low heat. Pour in the eggs.
  4. Scramble them gently. Stir slowly with a spatula, pushing the eggs around until just set and still a little shiny, about 3–4 minutes. Take them off the heat — they'll keep cooking.
  5. Warm the tortillas. Heat them in a dry pan or straight over a gas flame for a few seconds per side.
  6. Build the tacos. Fill each tortilla with eggs, sausage and cheddar. Top with salsa and sour cream.
