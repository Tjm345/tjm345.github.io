Thomas's White Queso Dip

Smooth, creamy white cheese dip just like the restaurants make. Stays melty in the crockpot all game long.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook15 minutes
Serves10 people
Nutritionabout 190 cal · 8g protein per serving

Keep it on warm, not low — low can scorch the cheese on the bottom.

What you need

  • 1 block white American cheese, cubed (one pound — from the deli counter)
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 can diced green chiles, drained (one small 4-ounce can)
  • 1 jalapeño, seeded and minced (optional, for heat)
  • Tortilla chips (for dipping)

Also grab: a crockpot or a heavy pot.

How to make it

  1. Start the cheese. Put the cheese cubes and milk in the crockpot on low.
  2. Stir it smooth. Stir every 5 minutes until the cheese is fully melted and smooth, about 15 minutes.
  3. Add the kick. Stir in the green chiles and jalapeño if using.
  4. Keep warm. Switch to the warm setting and serve with tortilla chips. Stir now and then.
