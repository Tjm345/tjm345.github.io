Thomas's Crispy Sweet Potato Fries

Crispy sweet potato fries with a cool dip. Sweet, salty, and hard to stop eating.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook25 minutes
Serves4 servings
Nutritionabout 220 cal · 3g protein per serving

Soak the cut fries in water 10 minutes if you have time — it helps them crisp.

What you need

  • 2 big sweet potatoes, cut into thin sticks
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika
  • 1/2 cup ranch dressing (for dipping)

Also grab: a baking sheet and a bowl.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 425 degrees.
  2. Toss the fries. In a bowl, toss the sweet potato sticks with olive oil, salt, and paprika.
  3. Spread them out. Spread on a baking sheet in one layer, with space between them.
  4. Bake. Bake 25 minutes, flipping halfway, till crispy at the edges.
  5. Serve. Serve warm with ranch for dipping.
