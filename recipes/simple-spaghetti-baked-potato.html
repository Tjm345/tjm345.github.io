Thomas's Simple Spaghetti & Baked Potato

Buttery baked potatoes and a big plate of spaghetti with marinara. Two comfort-food classics, one easy dinner.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook1 hour
Serves4 people
Nutritionabout 640 cal · 18g protein per serving

Start the potatoes first — they take the longest and the pasta comes together fast.

What you need

  • 4 big russet potatoes
  • 1 pound spaghetti (one box)
  • 1 jar marinara sauce, 24 ounces
  • 4 tablespoons butter (half a stick)
  • ½ cup grated parmesan cheese
  • 1 teaspoon salt, plus more for the pasta water
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • sour cream for the potatoes (optional)

Also grab: a big pot, a saucepan, a fork and a baking sheet if you want one (optional).

How to make it

  1. Bake the potatoes. Heat the oven to 400°F. Scrub the potatoes clean and poke each one a few times with a fork. Rub them with a little butter or oil and sprinkle with salt. Put them right on the oven rack and bake for about 1 hour, until the skin is crisp and a fork slides in easily.
  2. Boil the spaghetti. While the potatoes bake, bring a big pot of salted water to a rolling boil. Add the spaghetti and cook for the time on the box, usually 9–11 minutes, until tender but not mushy. Scoop out a cup of the pasta water, then drain.
  3. Warm the sauce. Pour the marinara into a saucepan over medium heat and warm it for about 5 minutes, stirring now and then, until it is steaming.
  4. Toss it together. Put the drained spaghetti back in the pot. Pour in the warm marinara, add 2 tablespoons of butter, the garlic powder and a splash of the pasta water. Toss until every strand is coated and glossy.
  5. Serve it up. Split each baked potato open, fluff the insides with a fork, and top with butter, salt, pepper and sour cream if you like. Plate the spaghetti next to it with parmesan on top.

How to serve it

Extra parmesan and a little black pepper over the spaghetti. Hot sauce on the potato if you like heat.

Save the leftovers

Refrigerate for up to 4 days. Reheat the pasta in a pan with a splash of water so the sauce loosens up.
