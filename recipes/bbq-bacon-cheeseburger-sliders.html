Thomas's BBQ Bacon Cheeseburger Sliders

Mini cheeseburgers with crispy bacon and tangy BBQ sauce on soft slider buns. Gone in about four minutes at any party.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook15 minutes
Serves6 people (12 sliders)
Nutritionabout 600 cal · 35g protein per serving (2 sliders)

Make the patties a little wider than the buns because they shrink as they cook.

What you need

  • 1½ pounds ground beef (about one and a half store packages)
  • 12 slider buns
  • 6 slices cheddar cheese, cut in half
  • 6 slices bacon, cooked crisp and halved
  • ½ cup BBQ sauce
  • Dill pickle slices
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon pepper

Also grab: a big pan or grill, a spatula and a plate.

How to make them

  1. Shape the patties. Divide the beef into 12 equal balls and flatten them into small patties slightly wider than the buns. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
  2. Cook the burgers. Put them in a hot pan or on the grill for 2–3 minutes per side.
  3. Add the cheese. During the last minute, top each patty with half a cheese slice and let it melt.
  4. Toast the buns. Put them cut-side down in the pan for 1 minute.
  5. Build the sliders. Stack a bottom bun, patty, bacon, a spoonful of BBQ sauce, pickles and the top bun.
