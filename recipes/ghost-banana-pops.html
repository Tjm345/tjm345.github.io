Thomas's Ghost Banana Pops

Frozen bananas dipped in white chocolate with little chocolate-chip faces. Spooky, creamy, and way easier than they look.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep20 minutes
CookNo-bake + freezing
Serves8 pops
Nutritionabout 180 cal · 2g protein per pop

Work fast once the bananas are cut — they get slippery as they thaw.

What you need

  • 4 bananas
  • 2 cups white chocolate chips (one 12-ounce bag is plenty)
  • 1 tablespoon coconut oil or vegetable oil
  • Mini chocolate chips (for the ghost faces)
  • 8 popsicle sticks

Also grab: popsicle sticks or lollipop sticks, a baking sheet, and parchment paper.

How to make it

  1. Prep the bananas. Peel the bananas, cut each one in half, and push a popsicle stick into the cut end of each half. Freeze 1 hour on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
  2. Melt the coating. Microwave the white chocolate chips with the oil in 20-second bursts, stirring until smooth.
  3. Dip them. Dip each frozen banana half in the white chocolate, turning to coat. Let the extra drip off.
  4. Make the faces. Before the coating hardens, press 2 mini chocolate chips in for eyes and 1 for a mouth.
  5. Freeze again. Put them back in the freezer 30 minutes until firm. Keep frozen until serving.
