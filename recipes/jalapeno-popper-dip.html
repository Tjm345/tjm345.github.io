Thomas's Jalapeño Popper Dip

Everything good about a jalapeño popper in one hot, creamy dip. Scoop it up with tortilla chips.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook20 minutes
Serves8 people
Nutritionabout 240 cal · 7g protein per serving

Wear gloves or wash your hands right after cutting jalapeños — don't touch your eyes.

What you need

  • 2 blocks cream cheese, softened (8 ounces each — leave them on the counter 30 minutes)
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese, divided (one 8-ounce bag)
  • 4 jalapeños, seeded and diced small (use less if you don't like heat)
  • 6 slices bacon, cooked and crumbled (or one small bag of real bacon bits)
  • Tortilla chips (for scooping)

Also grab: a square baking dish and a mixing bowl.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 375 degrees.
  2. Mix the base. Stir the cream cheese and sour cream in a bowl until smooth, then mix in 1 cup of the cheddar, the jalapeños, and half the bacon.
  3. Fill the dish. Spread the mixture in a square baking dish and top with the rest of the cheddar and bacon.
  4. Bake. Bake 20 minutes until hot and bubbly with golden spots on top.
  5. Serve. Let it cool 5 minutes so nobody burns their mouth, then serve with tortilla chips.
