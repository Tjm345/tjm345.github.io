Thomas's Sausage & Potato Breakfast Skillet

Crispy potatoes, savory sausage, and fluffy scrambled eggs all cooked in one big skillet.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook25 minutes
Serves4 servings
Nutritionabout 420 cal · 20g protein per serving

Don't stir the potatoes too much — leaving them alone is how they get crispy.

What you need

  • 1 pound breakfast sausage (one standard package)
  • 4 potatoes, cut into little cubes (about 1/2 inch pieces)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped (any color)
  • 2 tablespoons butter (or oil)
  • 6 eggs
  • 1/4 cup milk (whole or 2%)
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese (optional, for the top)

Also grab: a big skillet with a lid, a spatula, and a bowl.

How to make it

  1. Cook the sausage. In a big skillet on medium heat, cook the sausage, breaking it up, till browned. Scoop it out and set it aside.
  2. Crisp the potatoes. Melt the butter in the same skillet, add the potatoes, onion, and bell pepper, and cook about 15 minutes, stirring only now and then, till the potatoes are golden and soft inside.
  3. Add the sausage back. Stir the sausage back into the skillet.
  4. Scramble the eggs. In a bowl, whisk the eggs, milk, salt, and pepper, then pour over everything in the skillet.
  5. Cook the eggs. Stir gently till the eggs are just set and fluffy.
  6. Top with cheese. Sprinkle the cheese on top if you're using it, put the lid on for a minute till it melts, then serve.
