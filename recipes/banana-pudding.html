Thomas's Banana Pudding

Layers of vanilla pudding, bananas, and vanilla wafers. Cool, creamy, and classic.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep20 minutes
Cook2 hours chilling
Serves6 servings
Nutritionabout 300 cal · 5g protein per serving

Chill it at least 2 hours so the wafers soften up just right.

What you need

  • 1 box vanilla pudding mix (instant)
  • 2 cups cold milk (whole or 2%)
  • 1 tub whipped topping (or 2 cups whipped cream)
  • 3 bananas, sliced
  • 1 box vanilla wafers

Also grab: a bowl, a whisk, and a dish.

How to make it

  1. Make the pudding. Whisk the pudding mix and milk till thick.
  2. Fold in the topping. Fold in the whipped topping till smooth.
  3. Layer it. In a dish, layer wafers, bananas, and pudding. Repeat.
  4. Chill. Chill at least 2 hours.
  5. Serve. Top with a few wafers and serve cold.
