Thomas's Garlic Parmesan Fries

Crispy oven fries tossed in garlic butter and showered with parmesan and parsley. Restaurant fries without leaving the house.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Bake25–30 minutes
Serves4 servings
Nutritionabout 380 cal · 8g protein per serving

Toss the fries with the garlic butter while they are still hot so everything sticks.

What you need

  • 1 bag frozen French fries, 28–32 ounces (one store bag)
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • 4 tablespoons butter (half a stick)
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced (or 1 teaspoon garlic powder)
  • ¾ cup grated parmesan cheese
  • 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped (or 2 teaspoons dried)
  • mayo or aioli for dipping (optional)

Also grab: a large baking sheet, parchment paper, a small saucepan and a big mixing bowl.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F.. Line your baking sheet with parchment paper so the fries do not stick.
  2. Season the fries.. Dump the frozen fries into the big bowl. Drizzle with the olive oil, sprinkle on the salt and pepper, and toss until every fry is coated.
  3. Bake until crispy.. Spread the fries out in one layer on the baking sheet. Crowded fries steam instead of crisp, so use two sheets if you need to. Bake 25–30 minutes, flipping them once halfway through, until deep golden and crispy.
  4. Make the garlic butter.. While the fries bake, melt the butter in the small saucepan over low heat. Stir in the minced garlic and cook 1–2 minutes until it smells amazing. Do not let it brown.
  5. Toss it all together.. Put the hot fries back in the big bowl. Pour the garlic butter over them and toss well. Sprinkle on the parmesan and parsley and toss again.
  6. Serve right away.. Pile them into a basket or bowl and serve hot with mayo or aioli for dipping.

How to serve it

Best eaten hot and fresh. A squeeze of lemon on top is really good too.

Save the leftovers

Refrigerate for up to 2 days. Reheat in a 425°F oven for 8–10 minutes to crisp them back up. The microwave makes them soggy.
