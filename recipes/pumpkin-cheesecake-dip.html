Thomas's Pumpkin Cheesecake Dip

Everything you love about pumpkin cheesecake, turned into a fluffy dip. Graham crackers and apple slices required.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
CookNo-bake
Serves10 people
Nutritionabout 190 cal · 2g protein per serving

Serve it in a hollowed-out mini pumpkin for maximum fall vibes.

What you need

  • 1 block (8 ounces) cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup pumpkin puree
  • 1/2 cup powdered sugar
  • 1 teaspoon pumpkin pie spice
  • 1 tub (8 ounces) whipped topping, thawed
  • Graham crackers and apple slices (for dipping)

Also grab: a big bowl and an electric mixer or sturdy spoon.

How to make it

  1. Beat it smooth. Beat the cream cheese until smooth, then beat in the pumpkin, powdered sugar, and pumpkin pie spice.
  2. Fold in the fluff. Stir in the whipped topping gently until it's light and fluffy.
  3. Chill. Refrigerate 30 minutes so the flavors hang out together.
  4. Serve. Spoon into a bowl and surround with graham crackers and apple slices.
