Thomas's Peanut Butter Spider Cookies

Soft peanut butter cookies with a chocolate truffle body and pretzel legs. Crunchy, chocolatey, and creepy in the best way.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Cook12 minutes
Serves24 cookies
Nutritionabout 140 cal · 3g protein per cookie

Push the chocolate in while the cookies are hot — that's what holds the spiders together.

What you need

  • 1/2 cup butter, softened (one stick, room temp)
  • 1/2 cup peanut butter
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar, packed
  • 1 egg
  • 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3/4 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 24 chocolate truffles or round chocolates
  • 48 pretzel sticks, broken in half (for legs)
  • Candy eyes (optional)

Also grab: a baking sheet, parchment paper, and a bowl.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 350°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Make the dough. Beat the butter, peanut butter, and both sugars until fluffy. Beat in the egg, then stir in the flour, baking soda, and salt.
  3. Bake. Roll into 1-inch balls, place 2 inches apart, and bake 10–12 minutes until the edges are set.
  4. Build the spiders. Right out of the oven, press a chocolate truffle into the center of each cookie and stick 8 pretzel halves around it for legs. Add candy eyes if you want.
  5. Cool. Let them cool on the pan 10 minutes so the spiders stay put.
