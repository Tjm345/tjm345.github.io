Thomas's Halloween Chocolate Bark

Melted chocolate spread thin and loaded with candy corn, pretzels, and sprinkles, then broken into chunks. Fifteen minutes, zero skill needed.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
CookNo-bake
Serves12 pieces
Nutritionabout 170 cal · 2g protein per piece

Swirl two kinds of chocolate together for a marbled look.

What you need

  • 2 cups chocolate chips (half milk, half white is fun)
  • 1/2 cup candy corn
  • 1/2 cup mini pretzels, broken
  • 2 tablespoons Halloween sprinkles

Also grab: a baking sheet and parchment paper.

How to make it

  1. Set up. Line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Melt the chocolate. Microwave the chocolate chips in 20-second bursts, stirring until smooth.
  3. Spread it. Pour onto the parchment and spread about 1/4 inch thick with a spatula.
  4. Load the toppings. Scatter the candy corn, pretzel pieces, and sprinkles all over. Press them in lightly.
  5. Chill and break. Refrigerate 30 minutes until hard, then break into jagged pieces with your hands.
