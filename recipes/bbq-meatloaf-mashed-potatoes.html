Thomas's BBQ Meatloaf with Mashed Potatoes

Juicy meatloaf with a sweet BBQ glaze, served with creamy mashed potatoes. Classic comfort food.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Cook60 minutes
Serves6 servings
Nutritionabout 480 cal · 28g protein per serving

Let the meatloaf rest 10 minutes before slicing so it doesn't fall apart.

What you need

  • 2 pounds ground beef
  • 1 onion, chopped small
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs (plain)
  • 1/2 cup milk (whole or 2%)
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce (optional, the dark sauce bottle)
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 cup BBQ sauce (divided — half in the mix, half on top)
  • 4 potatoes, peeled and cut up (for the mashed potatoes)
  • 4 tablespoons butter (for the mashed potatoes)
  • 1/2 cup milk (for the mashed potatoes)

Also grab: a big bowl, a loaf pan or baking dish, and a pot for the potatoes.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 375 degrees.
  2. Mix the meatloaf. In a big bowl, mix the beef, onion, eggs, breadcrumbs, milk, Worcestershire if using, salt, pepper, and half the BBQ sauce. Don't overmix.
  3. Shape and glaze. Press the mix into a loaf pan or shape a loaf in a baking dish, and spread the rest of the BBQ sauce on top.
  4. Bake. Bake about 1 hour, till cooked through in the middle.
  5. Make the mashed potatoes. While it bakes, boil the potatoes till soft, drain, and mash with butter and milk till creamy.
  6. Rest and serve. Let the meatloaf rest 10 minutes, slice, and serve with the mashed potatoes.
