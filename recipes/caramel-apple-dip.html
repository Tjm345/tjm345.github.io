Thomas's Caramel Apple Dip

Creamy caramel dip with toffee bits on top. Apple slices basically become a vehicle for this stuff.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
CookNo-bake
Serves10 people
Nutritionabout 180 cal · 2g protein per serving

Toss cut apples in a little lemon juice so they don't turn brown on the plate.

What you need

  • 1 block (8 ounces) cream cheese, softened
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar, packed
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1 tub (8 ounces) whipped topping, thawed
  • 1/2 cup caramel sauce
  • 1/4 cup toffee bits
  • Apple slices (for dipping — lots of them)

Also grab: a bowl and a mixer or spoon.

How to make it

  1. Beat the base. Beat the cream cheese, brown sugar, and vanilla until smooth.
  2. Fold it together. Stir in the whipped topping until fluffy.
  3. Top it. Spread into a serving bowl, drizzle the caramel sauce over the top, and sprinkle with toffee bits.
  4. Serve. Put out a mountain of apple slices and dig in.
