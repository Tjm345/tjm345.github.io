Thomas's Creamy Tuscan Salmon

Seared salmon in a creamy garlic sauce with spinach and sun-dried tomatoes. Fancy-tasting but easy.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook20 minutes
Serves4 servings
Nutritionabout 420 cal · 34g protein per serving

Don't let the cream boil hard — a gentle simmer keeps it smooth.

What you need

  • 4 salmon fillets (skin on or off)
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced (or 1 teaspoon garlic powder)
  • 1 cup heavy cream (the small carton)
  • 1/2 cup sun-dried tomatoes, chopped (from a jar, drained)
  • 2 cups baby spinach (two big handfuls)
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese

How to make it

  1. Season the salmon. Sprinkle salt and pepper on the salmon fillets.
  2. Sear the salmon. Melt the butter in a big pan on medium-high heat, add the salmon skin-side down if it has skin, and cook about 4 minutes per side till golden. Set aside.
  3. Start the sauce. In the same pan, cook the garlic 1 minute, then pour in the cream.
  4. Add the good stuff. Stir in the sun-dried tomatoes, spinach, and Parmesan, and simmer gently till the spinach wilts and the sauce thickens a little.
  5. Finish and serve. Put the salmon back in the pan, spoon sauce over it, and serve.
