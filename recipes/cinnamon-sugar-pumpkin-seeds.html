Thomas's Cinnamon Sugar Pumpkin Seeds

Don't throw out the guts when you carve a pumpkin — roast the seeds with cinnamon sugar for the best fall snack.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook30 minutes
Serves2 cups
Nutritionabout 150 cal · 7g protein per 1/4 cup

Dry the seeds well — wet seeds steam instead of crisping.

What you need

  • 2 cups raw pumpkin seeds, rinsed and dried (from about 1 big pumpkin)
  • 2 tablespoons butter, melted
  • 3 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt

Also grab: a baking sheet, parchment paper, and a bowl.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 300°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Dry the seeds. Rinse the seeds, pick off the stringy bits, and pat them very dry with a towel.
  3. Season. Toss the seeds with melted butter, then sprinkle the sugar, cinnamon, and salt over them and toss again.
  4. Roast. Spread in one layer and bake 25–30 minutes, stirring once halfway, until golden and crisp.
  5. Cool. Let them cool on the pan — they crisp up more as they cool.
