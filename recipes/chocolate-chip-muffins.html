Recipe 144 · Dessert

Thomas's Chocolate Chip Muffins

Soft muffins packed with chocolate chips. Warm from the oven, they're hard to beat.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook20 minutes
Serves12 muffins
Nutritionabout 220 cal · 4g protein per muffin

Don't overmix the batter — stir till just mixed for soft muffins.

What you need

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup milk (whole or 2%)
  • 1/3 cup oil (vegetable)
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1 cup chocolate chips

Also grab: a muffin tin, a bowl, and a spoon.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 375 degrees.
  2. Mix the dry stuff. In a bowl, mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
  3. Mix the wet stuff. In another bowl, mix the milk, oil, egg, and vanilla.
  4. Combine. Pour the wet into the dry and stir till just mixed, then fold in the chocolate chips.
  5. Bake. Fill the muffin cups 3/4 full and bake 20 minutes till golden.
  6. Cool and serve. Cool a few minutes and serve warm.
