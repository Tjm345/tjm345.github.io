Thomas's Tuna Melt Sandwiches

Creamy tuna salad and gooey melted cheddar grilled golden on buttery bread. The tuna sandwich grew up and got delicious.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook10 minutes
Serves4 sandwiches
Nutritionabout 560 cal · 32g protein per sandwich

Press down gently with the spatula so the cheese melts all the way through.

What you need

  • 2 cans tuna in water, 5 ounces each (drained well)
  • ½ cup mayonnaise
  • 2 tablespoons sweet pickle relish
  • ¼ cup diced celery (about 1 stalk)
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • 8 slices sandwich bread
  • 4 slices cheddar cheese (or 1 cup shredded)
  • 4 tablespoons butter, softened

Also grab: a large skillet, a mixing bowl and a spatula.

How to make it

  1. Mix the tuna salad.. Put the drained tuna in the bowl and flake it apart with a fork. Stir in the mayo, relish, celery, salt and pepper.
  2. Butter the bread.. Spread softened butter on one side of every slice of bread — this side goes face-down in the pan.
  3. Build the melts.. On the unbuttered side of 4 slices, pile on a quarter of the tuna salad and lay a slice of cheddar over the top. Cap with the other bread, buttered side out.
  4. Grill low and slow.. Cook over medium-low heat 3–4 minutes per side, pressing gently, until the bread is deep golden and the cheese is fully melted.
  5. Cut and serve.. Let them rest a minute, then cut in half so the cheese doesn't all run out.

How to serve it

Pickle chips and a bowl of tomato soup — just like the diner.

Save the leftovers

Best fresh. Leftover tuna salad (without bread) keeps 3 days refrigerated in a sealed container.
