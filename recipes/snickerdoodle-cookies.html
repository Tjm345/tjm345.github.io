Thomas's Snickerdoodle Cookies

Soft, chewy cookies rolled in cinnamon sugar with those classic crackled tops. The kind of cookie that disappears fast.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Bake10–12 minutes
Serves24 cookies
Nutritionabout 120 cal · 1g protein per cookie

Do not overbake them — they should look a little soft in the middle when you pull them out.

What you need

  • 2¾ cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons cream of tartar (in the baking aisle)
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup butter, softened (two sticks, leave out 30 minutes)
  • 1½ cups sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • ¼ cup sugar for rolling
  • 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon for rolling

Also grab: two baking sheets, parchment paper or cooking spray, two mixing bowls and a mixer.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F.. Line two baking sheets with parchment paper so nothing sticks.
  2. Mix the dry ingredients.. Whisk the flour, cream of tartar, baking soda and salt together in a bowl. Cream of tartar is what gives snickerdoodles their classic tangy chew.
  3. Cream the butter and sugar.. Beat the softened butter and sugar together with the mixer for about 2 minutes until light and fluffy. Then beat in the eggs one at a time and the vanilla.
  4. Bring it together.. Add the dry mixture to the wet mixture a little at a time, mixing on low just until combined. Do not overmix.
  5. Roll in cinnamon sugar.. Stir the 1/4 cup sugar and cinnamon together in a small bowl. Scoop the dough into balls about the size of a golf ball and roll each one in the cinnamon sugar.
  6. Bake 10–12 minutes.. Place the balls 2 inches apart on the sheets. Bake until the edges are set and the tops are crackled but the centers still look a little soft.
  7. Cool on the pan.. Let them sit on the hot pan for 5 minutes to finish setting, then move to a rack.

How to serve it

Best warm with a cold glass of milk. These are perfect for bake sales and cookie swaps.

Save the leftovers

Keep in a sealed container at room temperature for up to 5 days. They stay soft for days.
