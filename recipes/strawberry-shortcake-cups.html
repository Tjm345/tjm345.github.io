Thomas's Strawberry Shortcake Cups

Soft cake, sweet strawberries, and whipped cream layered in cups. Easy, pretty, and no fork needed.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
CookNo cooking
Serves6 cups
Nutritionabout 280 cal · 3g protein per cup

Let the berries sit with the sugar 10 minutes — they make their own syrup.

What you need

  • 1 pound cake, store-bought (sliced into cubes)
  • 2 cups strawberries, sliced (one box)
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 cup heavy cream (or 1 tub whipped topping)
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla (if whipping your own cream)

Also grab: a bowl, a whisk, and 6 cups or jars.

How to make it

  1. Sweeten the berries. In a bowl, mix the sliced strawberries and sugar, and let sit 10 minutes.
  2. Whip the cream. If using heavy cream, whip it with the vanilla till soft peaks. If using whipped topping, skip this.
  3. Layer the cups. In each cup, layer cake cubes, berries with their syrup, and whipped cream. Repeat once.
  4. Serve. Serve right away, or chill up to 2 hours.
