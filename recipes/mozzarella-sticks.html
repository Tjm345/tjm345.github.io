Thomas's Mozzarella Sticks

Crispy, cheesy mozzarella sticks with marinara for dipping. The appetizer everyone grabs first.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Cook10 minutes
Serves4 servings
Nutritionabout 320 cal · 16g protein per serving

Freeze the cheese sticks 20 minutes before coating so the cheese doesn't leak out.

What you need

  • 12 mozzarella string cheese sticks
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs (plain or Italian)
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • Oil for frying, or cooking spray for baking
  • Marinara sauce for dipping

Also grab: 3 bowls, a pan, and a plate.

How to make it

  1. Freeze the cheese. Put the string cheese in the freezer 20 minutes.
  2. Set up the coating. Put flour in one bowl, beaten eggs in another, and breadcrumbs mixed with garlic powder and salt in a third.
  3. Coat twice. Roll each stick in flour, then egg, then breadcrumbs. Repeat the egg and breadcrumbs for a second coat.
  4. Cook. Fry in hot oil 1–2 minutes till golden, or bake at 425 degrees for 10 minutes with a spray of oil.
  5. Serve. Serve warm with marinara.
