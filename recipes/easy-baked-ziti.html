Thomas's Easy Baked Ziti

Tender ziti noodles baked in marinara with creamy ricotta and a blanket of bubbly mozzarella. The coziest pan in the oven.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Bake30 minutes
Serves8 servings
Nutritionabout 640 cal · 30g protein per serving

Let it rest 10 minutes before scooping so the slices hold together.

What you need

  • 1 pound ziti pasta (one 16-ounce box)
  • 1 pound ground beef (one store package)
  • 1 jar marinara sauce, 24 ounces
  • 1 container ricotta cheese, 15 ounces
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese (about one and a half 8-ounce bags)
  • ½ cup grated parmesan cheese
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt

Also grab: a large pot, a large skillet, a 9x13 baking dish and a mixing bowl.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F.. Grease your baking dish so nothing sticks.
  2. Brown the beef.. Cook the ground beef in the skillet over medium-high heat for 8–10 minutes, breaking it up, until no pink remains. Drain off the grease.
  3. Add the sauce.. Stir the marinara into the beef and let it simmer on low while you do the next step.
  4. Boil the ziti short.. Cook the pasta in salted boiling water for 2 minutes LESS than the box says. It finishes cooking in the oven, so undercooking keeps it from getting mushy. Drain.
  5. Mix the ricotta.. In the bowl, stir together the ricotta, egg, parmesan, Italian seasoning, garlic powder and salt.
  6. Layer it up.. Spread half the pasta in the dish, dollop over half the ricotta mix, spoon over half the meat sauce and sprinkle with a third of the mozzarella. Repeat, ending with the rest of the mozzarella on top.
  7. Bake 25–30 minutes.. It is done when the cheese is melted, bubbly and starting to brown at the edges. Rest 10 minutes before scooping.

How to serve it

Garlic bread for scooping up the extra sauce, and a green salad on the side.

Save the leftovers

Refrigerate up to 4 days. Reheats great in the microwave or oven.
