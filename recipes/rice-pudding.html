Thomas's Rice Pudding

Creamy, sweet rice pudding with cinnamon. Old-fashioned comfort in a bowl.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep5 minutes
Cook30 minutes
Serves4 servings
Nutritionabout 280 cal · 7g protein per serving

Stir often so the rice doesn't stick to the bottom.

What you need

  • 1/2 cup rice (short or medium grain)
  • 2 cups milk (whole or 2%)
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • Pinch salt

Also grab: a pot and a spoon.

How to make it

  1. Mix it up. In a pot, mix the rice, milk, sugar, and salt.
  2. Cook low and slow. Cook over low heat 30 minutes, stirring often, till thick and creamy.
  3. Add vanilla. Stir in the vanilla and cinnamon.
  4. Serve. Serve warm, or chill it for cold rice pudding.
