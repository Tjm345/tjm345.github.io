Thomas's Baked Zucchini Fries

Crispy zucchini sticks with a crunchy coating. A lighter fry that still crunches.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Cook20 minutes
Serves4 servings
Nutritionabout 160 cal · 5g protein per serving

Pat the zucchini dry first — wet zucchini won't crisp.

What you need

  • 2 zucchini, cut into sticks
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs (plain or panko)
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • Ranch dressing for dipping, optional

Also grab: a baking sheet and 2 bowls.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 425 degrees.
  2. Mix the coating. In a bowl, mix the breadcrumbs, Parmesan, garlic powder, and salt.
  3. Coat the sticks. Dip each zucchini stick in the beaten egg, then roll in the coating.
  4. Bake. Spread on a baking sheet and bake 20 minutes, flipping halfway, till golden and crispy.
  5. Serve. Serve warm with ranch if you like.
