Thomas's Creamy Broccoli Cheddar Soup

Thick, cheesy soup loaded with broccoli. Warm, cozy, and great with bread for dipping.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook25 minutes
Serves6 bowls
Nutritionabout 310 cal · 14g protein per bowl

Stir the cheese in off the heat so it melts smooth, not clumpy.

What you need

  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced (or 1 teaspoon garlic powder)
  • 4 cups broccoli florets (one big bag, fresh or frozen)
  • 4 cups chicken broth (one big box)
  • 2 cups milk (whole or 2%)
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • 3 cups shredded cheddar cheese (one 12-ounce bag)
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

Also grab: a big pot, a spoon, and a potato masher (optional).

How to make it

  1. Cook the onion. Melt the butter in a big pot, add the onion, and cook till soft, about 5 minutes.
  2. Add garlic and flour. Stir in the garlic, then the flour, and cook 1 minute, stirring.
  3. Pour in the liquids. Pour in the broth and milk slowly, stirring so no lumps form.
  4. Add the broccoli. Add the broccoli and cook about 15 minutes, till soft.
  5. Mash a little. Mash some of the broccoli with a masher right in the pot, or leave it chunky if you like it that way.
  6. Melt the cheese. Turn the heat low and stir in the cheese a handful at a time till melted. Add salt and pepper, then serve.
