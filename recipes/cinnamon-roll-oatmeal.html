Thomas's Cinnamon Roll Oatmeal

Warm oatmeal that tastes like a cinnamon roll, with a sweet icing swirl on top. Cozy, easy, and ready in minutes.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep5 minutes
Cook10 minutes
Serves4 bowls
Nutritionabout 320 cal · 8g protein per bowl

Swirl the icing on right before you eat — it melts into the warm oats.

What you need

  • 2 cups old-fashioned rolled oats (the round can kind)
  • 2 cups milk (whole or 2%)
  • 2 cups water
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar (packed down in the cup)
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla (about half a little capful)
  • A pinch salt
  • 1/2 cup powdered sugar (for the icing)
  • 2 tablespoons milk (for the icing)

Also grab: a pot, a spoon, and a little bowl for the icing.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oats. Put the oats, milk, water, brown sugar, cinnamon, and salt in a pot.
  2. Cook and stir. Cook on medium heat, stirring a lot, for about 8 to 10 minutes, till thick and creamy.
  3. Add the vanilla. Take the pot off the heat and stir in the vanilla.
  4. Make the icing. In a little bowl, mix the powdered sugar and milk till smooth.
  5. Serve. Spoon the oatmeal into bowls and drizzle the icing on top in a swirl.
