Thomas's Caprese Grilled Cheese

Melty mozzarella, tomato, and basil pressed in buttery bread. Like a grilled cheese went on vacation.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook10 minutes
Serves2 sandwiches
Nutritionabout 420 cal · 18g protein per sandwich

Keep the heat medium — low and slow melts the cheese without burning the bread.

What you need

  • 4 slices bread (sourdough or white)
  • 4 slices mozzarella cheese (fresh or regular)
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 8 basil leaves (fresh)
  • 2 tablespoons butter (softened)
  • 1 tablespoon balsamic glaze (optional, the sweet dark drizzle)

Also grab: a pan and a spatula.

How to make it

  1. Build the sandwiches. On 2 slices of bread, layer mozzarella, tomato slices, and basil. Top with the other slices.
  2. Butter the outside. Spread butter on the outside of each sandwich.
  3. Cook. Cook in a pan over medium heat, 3–4 minutes per side, till golden and the cheese melts.
  4. Serve. Cut in half, drizzle with balsamic glaze if you like, and serve warm.
