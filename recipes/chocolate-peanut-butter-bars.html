Thomas's Chocolate Peanut Butter Bars

Thick peanut butter bars with a chocolate top. No baking, just mix, chill, and cut.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Cook1 hour chilling
Serves16 bars
Nutritionabout 290 cal · 6g protein per bar

Score the chocolate lightly before it sets all the way — clean cuts.

What you need

  • 1 cup peanut butter (creamy)
  • 1/2 cup butter, melted
  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 2 cups graham cracker crumbs (one sleeve, crushed)
  • 1 1/2 cups chocolate chips
  • 2 tablespoons butter (for the chocolate top)

Also grab: a bowl, a pan (8x8), and a spoon.

How to make it

  1. Mix the base. In a bowl, mix the peanut butter, melted butter, powdered sugar, and graham crumbs till it holds together.
  2. Press into the pan. Press the mix flat into the pan.
  3. Melt the chocolate. Melt the chocolate chips with the 2 tablespoons butter in the microwave in short bursts, stirring.
  4. Top and chill. Spread the chocolate over the base and chill 1 hour till firm.
  5. Cut and serve. Cut into bars and serve.
