Thomas's Ham, Egg & Cheese Breakfast Quesadillas

Crispy golden tortillas stuffed with fluffy scrambled eggs, diced ham and melty cheddar. Breakfast you can hold in your hand.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook15 minutes
Serves4 quesadillas
Nutritionabout 480 cal · 26g protein per quesadilla

Cook until both sides are deep golden and the cheese is fully melted.

What you need

  • 8 large flour tortillas (10-inch) (one store package)
  • 8 large eggs
  • 1 cup diced ham (one 8-ounce package)
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese (one 8-ounce bag)
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • salsa for serving (optional)

Also grab: a large skillet, a mixing bowl, a whisk and a spatula.

How to make it

  1. Whisk the eggs.. Crack all 8 eggs into a large bowl. Add the salt and pepper and whisk until the yolks and whites are fully mixed.
  2. Scramble them soft.. Melt 1 tablespoon of butter in the skillet over medium heat. Pour in the eggs and stir gently with the spatula for 3–4 minutes, until just set but still soft. Slide them onto a plate.
  3. Build the quesadillas.. Wipe out the skillet. Lay a tortilla flat and cover one half with a quarter of the cheese, a quarter of the ham, a quarter of the eggs and a little more cheese. Fold the empty half over the top.
  4. Cook until golden.. Melt a little of the remaining butter in the skillet over medium heat. Cook the quesadilla 2–3 minutes per side, until deep golden and the cheese is melted. Repeat with the rest.
  5. Cut and serve.. Cut each quesadilla into 3 wedges and serve hot with salsa if you like.

How to serve it

Salsa, hot sauce or sour cream on the side. These are great for busy mornings.

Save the leftovers

Refrigerate for up to 3 days. Reheat in a dry skillet so they crisp back up.
