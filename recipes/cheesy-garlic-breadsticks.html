Thomas's Cheesy Garlic Breadsticks

Soft pizza-dough breadsticks loaded with garlic butter and melted mozzarella. Made for dunking in marinara.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Cook15 minutes
Serves8 breadsticks
Nutritionabout 210 cal · 7g protein per breadstick

Brush the garlic butter on the second the breadsticks come out — it soaks right in.

What you need

  • 1 can refrigerated pizza dough (one 13.8-ounce can)
  • 4 tablespoons butter, melted (half a stick)
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced (or 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder)
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Marinara sauce, warmed (for dipping)

Also grab: a baking sheet and parchment paper.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 400 degrees and line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Shape them. Unroll the dough and cut it into 8 long strips. Twist each one once and lay on the sheet.
  3. Garlic butter. Stir the garlic into the melted butter and brush it all over the strips.
  4. Cheese it. Sprinkle mozzarella over every strip.
  5. Bake and dunk. Bake 12 to 15 minutes until golden. Serve hot with warm marinara.
