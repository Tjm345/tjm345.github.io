Thomas's Cheesy Broccoli Bites

Little baked bites of broccoli and cheese. A snack kids and grown-ups both grab.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Cook20 minutes
Serves4 servings
Nutritionabout 180 cal · 9g protein per serving

Squeeze the cooked broccoli dry so the bites hold together.

What you need

  • 2 cups broccoli, cooked and chopped small
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • Ranch dressing for dipping, optional

Also grab: a baking sheet, a bowl, and a spoon.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 375 degrees.
  2. Mix it up. In a bowl, mix the broccoli, cheese, breadcrumbs, eggs, garlic powder, and salt.
  3. Shape the bites. Roll into small balls and place on a baking sheet.
  4. Bake. Bake 20 minutes till golden.
  5. Serve. Serve warm with ranch if you like.
