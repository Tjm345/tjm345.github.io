Thomas's Chicken Enchiladas

Rolled tortillas filled with chicken and cheese, baked in red sauce. Comforting and cheesy.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep20 minutes
Cook25 minutes
Serves4 servings
Nutritionabout 540 cal · 36g protein per serving

Warm the tortillas first so they roll without cracking.

What you need

  • 2 cups cooked chicken, shredded
  • 1 can enchilada sauce (red)
  • 8 flour tortillas
  • 2 cups shredded cheese (Mexican blend or cheddar)
  • 1 can black beans, drained (optional)
  • 1/2 cup sour cream (for serving)

Also grab: a baking dish and a spoon.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 375 degrees.
  2. Fill the tortillas. Mix the chicken, half the cheese, and beans if using. Spoon into tortillas and roll them up.
  3. Arrange and sauce. Place seam-side down in the dish, pour the sauce over, and sprinkle the rest of the cheese on top.
  4. Bake. Bake 25 minutes till bubbly.
  5. Serve. Serve warm with sour cream.
