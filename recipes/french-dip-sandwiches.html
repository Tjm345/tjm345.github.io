Thomas's French Dip Sandwiches

Warm roast beef and melty provolone on toasted hoagie rolls, with hot beef broth for dunking. Fancy but easy.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook20 minutes
Serves4 sandwiches
Nutritionabout 540 cal · 34g protein per sandwich

Toast the rolls cut-side up — they hold together better when you dunk them.

What you need

  • 4 hoagie rolls
  • 1 pound deli roast beef, sliced thin (from the deli counter)
  • 4 slices provolone cheese
  • 2 cups beef broth (one 14-ounce can is fine)
  • 2 tablespoons butter, softened

Also grab: a baking sheet and a small pot.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 400 degrees.
  2. Prep the rolls. Split the rolls, butter the insides, and lay them cut-side up on the baking sheet.
  3. Fill them. Pile roast beef on each roll and lay a slice of provolone on top.
  4. Toast. Bake 8 to 10 minutes until the cheese is melty and the rolls are toasty.
  5. Make the dunk. While they bake, heat the beef broth in the small pot until steaming. Pour into little bowls and dunk away.
