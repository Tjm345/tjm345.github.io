Thomas's Chicken Parmesan

Crispy breaded chicken smothered in marinara and melted mozzarella, served over spaghetti. Restaurant-style, made easy.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep20 minutes
Cook25 minutes
Serves4 servings
Nutritionabout 780 cal · 52g protein per serving

Bake until the cheese is bubbly and golden at the edges.

What you need

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (one store package)
  • 1 cup Italian-seasoned breadcrumbs
  • ½ cup grated parmesan cheese
  • 2 large eggs
  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 jar marinara sauce, 24 ounces
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese (one 8-ounce bag)
  • ½ teaspoon each salt, black pepper and garlic powder
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • 8 ounces spaghetti (half a 16-ounce box)
  • fresh basil (optional)

Also grab: a large skillet, a baking dish, three shallow bowls, tongs and a pot for pasta.

How to make it

  1. Pound the chicken even.. Put each chicken breast in a zip-top bag and pound it with a heavy pan or rolling pin until about ½ inch thick. Even thickness means even cooking.
  2. Set up your breading station.. Put the flour in one shallow bowl, beat the eggs in a second bowl, and mix the breadcrumbs, parmesan and the salt-pepper-garlic mix in a third.
  3. Bread the chicken.. Coat each breast in flour, dip it in the beaten eggs, then press it into the breadcrumb mix until it is fully covered.
  4. Fry until golden.. Heat the olive oil in the skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken 3–4 minutes per side until deep golden. It does not need to be cooked through yet.
  5. Top and bake.. Heat the oven to 400°F. Spread 1 cup of marinara in the baking dish, lay in the chicken, spoon the rest of the sauce over the top and cover with mozzarella. Bake 15 minutes until the cheese is bubbly and golden.
  6. Boil the spaghetti.. While the chicken bakes, cook the spaghetti in a big pot of salted boiling water until tender, then drain.
  7. Serve it up.. Lay the chicken over the spaghetti and tear fresh basil over the top if you like.

How to serve it

A simple side salad and garlic bread make it a full Italian night.

Save the leftovers

Refrigerate up to 3 days. Reheat in a 350°F oven so the breading stays crisp.
