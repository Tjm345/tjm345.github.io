Thomas's Apple Cider Donut Muffins

All the cinnamon-sugar joy of an apple cider donut, baked into a soft muffin. Way easier than frying donuts.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook20 minutes
Serves12 muffins
Nutritionabout 180 cal · 2g protein per muffin

Roll them in the cinnamon sugar while they're warm — it sticks way better.

What you need

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon nutmeg
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup apple cider (the cloudy kind from the jug)
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 cup butter, melted
  • 1/2 cup sugar mixed with 1 teaspoon cinnamon (for rolling)
  • 3 tablespoons butter, melted (for rolling)

Also grab: a 12-cup muffin tin, paper liners, and two bowls.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 350°F. Line a 12-cup muffin tin with paper liners.
  2. Mix the dry stuff. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt in a big bowl.
  3. Mix the wet stuff. In another bowl, whisk the cider, milk, egg, and melted butter.
  4. Combine and bake. Stir the wet into the dry just until mixed. Fill the cups three-quarters full and bake 18–20 minutes.
  5. Sugar-coat them. While warm, brush each muffin with melted butter and roll in the cinnamon sugar.
