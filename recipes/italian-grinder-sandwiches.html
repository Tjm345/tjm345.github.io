Thomas's Italian Grinder Sandwiches

A deli-style sandwich piled with meats, cheese, lettuce, tomato, and a tangy dressing on a crusty roll.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
CookNo cooking
Serves4 sandwiches
Nutritionabout 480 cal · 24g protein per sandwich

Hollow out a little bread from the roll so all the fillings fit.

What you need

  • 4 hoagie rolls (the crusty kind)
  • 1/2 pound sliced ham
  • 1/2 pound sliced salami
  • 1/2 pound sliced turkey
  • 8 slices provolone cheese
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • 1/4 red onion, sliced thin
  • 1/4 cup Italian dressing (from a bottle)
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning (optional)

Also grab: a knife and a cutting board.

How to make it

  1. Prep the rolls. Cut each roll open, but not all the way through, so it opens like a book.
  2. Dress it. Drizzle Italian dressing inside each roll.
  3. Layer the meats and cheese. Layer ham, salami, turkey, and provolone in each roll.
  4. Add the veggies. Add tomato, lettuce, and red onion on top.
  5. Season and serve. Sprinkle Italian seasoning if using, close the sandwiches, and cut in half.
