Recipe 62 · Dessert

Thomas's Lemon Bars

Buttery shortbread crust under a bright, tangy lemon filling with a dusting of powdered sugar. Sweet, sour and gone in no time.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Bake35–40 minutes
Serves16 bars
Nutritionabout 200 cal · 3g protein per bar

The filling is done when the center barely jiggles — it firms up as it cools.

What you need

  • 1 cup butter, softened (2 sticks)
  • ½ cup powdered sugar, plus extra for dusting
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1½ cups granulated sugar
  • ½ cup fresh lemon juice (about 3–4 lemons)
  • 1 tablespoon grated lemon zest (from those same lemons)
  • ¼ cup all-purpose flour

Also grab: a 9x13-inch baking pan, parchment paper, a mixing bowl and a whisk.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F.. Line the baking pan with parchment paper, leaving some hanging over the edges so you can lift the bars out later.
  2. Make the crust.. Beat the softened butter and powdered sugar together until creamy. Stir in the 2 cups of flour and the salt until it looks like wet sand.
  3. Press and bake the crust.. Press the mixture firmly and evenly into the bottom of the pan. Bake for 20 minutes, until the edges just start to turn golden.
  4. Whisk the lemon filling.. While the crust bakes, whisk the eggs, granulated sugar, lemon juice, lemon zest and the 1/4 cup of flour together until smooth.
  5. Pour and bake again.. Pour the filling over the hot crust. Bake 15–20 more minutes, until the center barely jiggles when you nudge the pan.
  6. Cool, then chill.. Let it cool on the counter for an hour, then refrigerate at least 2 hours. Cold bars cut way cleaner.
  7. Dust and cut.. Lift the whole slab out with the parchment. Dust the top with powdered sugar, then cut into 16 bars with a sharp knife.

How to serve it

Cold, straight from the fridge, with extra powdered sugar on top.

Save the leftovers

Keep covered in the fridge for up to 5 days. They taste even better the next day.
