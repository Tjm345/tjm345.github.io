Thomas's Classic Vanilla Cupcakes

Fluffy vanilla cupcakes with creamy homemade buttercream and a pile of sprinkles. Birthday-party perfect, any day of the week.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep20 minutes
Bake18–20 minutes
Serves12 cupcakes
Nutritionabout 340 cal · 3g protein per cupcake

Frost them only after they are completely cool or the frosting will melt right off.

What you need

  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1½ teaspoons baking powder
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • ½ cup butter, softened (one stick, leave out 30 minutes)
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 large eggs, at room temperature
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • ½ cup milk
  • 1 cup powdered sugar (for the frosting)
  • ½ cup butter, softened (one stick, for the frosting)
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract (for the frosting)
  • sprinkles

Also grab: a 12-cup muffin tin, paper liners, two mixing bowls, a mixer and a spoon or piping bag.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F.. Put paper liners in all 12 cups of the muffin tin.
  2. Mix the dry ingredients.. Whisk the flour, baking powder and salt together in a bowl and set it aside.
  3. Cream the butter and sugar.. Beat the 1/2 cup softened butter and the sugar with the mixer for about 3 minutes until pale and fluffy.
  4. Add the eggs and vanilla.. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla. Scrape down the sides of the bowl with a spoon.
  5. Finish the batter.. Add half the flour mixture, then the milk, then the rest of the flour. Mix on low just until smooth. Do not overmix or the cupcakes get tough.
  6. Fill and bake.. Divide the batter between the 12 cups, filling each about two-thirds full. Bake 18–20 minutes, until a toothpick poked in the middle comes out clean.
  7. Cool completely.. Let them cool in the tin for 5 minutes, then move to a rack. They must be fully cool before frosting.
  8. Make the frosting.. Beat the remaining 1/2 cup butter until smooth, then beat in the powdered sugar a little at a time, then the vanilla. Spread or pipe onto the cupcakes and shower with sprinkles.

How to serve it

Serve at room temperature. These are the cupcake everyone asks for at parties.

Save the leftovers

Keep in a sealed container at room temperature for 2 days, or refrigerate for up to 4 days.
