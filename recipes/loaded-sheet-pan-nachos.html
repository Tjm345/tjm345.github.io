Thomas's Loaded Sheet Pan Nachos

Tortilla chips piled high with seasoned beef, melted cheese and all the fixings—built for sharing.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook15 minutes
TotalAbout 25 minutes
Serves4–6 people
Nutritionabout 700 cal · 30g protein per serving

Spread the chips in one layer so every one gets beef and melted cheese.

What you need

  • 1 large bag tortilla chips
  • ½ pound ground beef
  • 1 packet taco seasoning (or 1 teaspoon each chili powder, cumin and garlic powder)
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar or Monterey Jack cheese
  • For the toppings
  • Diced tomato
  • Sliced jalapeños
  • Sour cream
  • Salsa
  • Sliced green onions

Also grab: a large skillet and a sheet pan.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F.
  2. Cook the beef. Brown it in a large skillet for 8–10 minutes, then drain the grease. Stir in the taco seasoning with a splash of water.
  3. Spread out the chips. Put them in a single layer on the sheet pan.
  4. Add the hot toppings. Spoon the seasoned beef over the chips and cover everything with shredded cheese.
  5. Bake for 8–10 minutes until the cheese is melted.
  6. Finish the nachos. Add the tomato, jalapeños, green onions, sour cream and salsa, then serve right away.

Load every chip

Use one layer of chips so the toppings don't all land on the ones at the top.

Best served fresh

These are best hot from the oven, before the chips start to soften.
