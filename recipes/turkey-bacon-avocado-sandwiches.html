Thomas's Turkey Bacon Avocado Sandwiches

Toasted bread stacked with turkey, crispy bacon, creamy avocado, and fresh tomato. A simple sandwich that feels like a treat.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook10 minutes
Serves4 sandwiches
Nutritionabout 420 cal · 26g protein per sandwich

Toast the bread well — it holds up better against the avocado.

What you need

  • 8 slices bread (toasted)
  • 8 slices bacon (cooked till crispy)
  • 1 pound sliced turkey (one standard package)
  • 2 avocados, sliced (ripe and soft)
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 4 lettuce leaves
  • 4 tablespoons mayonnaise (or spread)
  • A pinch salt and pepper (a pinch of each)

Also grab: a pan for the bacon, a toaster, and a knife.

How to make it

  1. Cook the bacon. Cook the bacon in a pan on medium heat till crispy, then set it on a paper towel.
  2. Toast the bread. Toast all 8 slices till golden.
  3. Spread. Spread mayonnaise on one side of each slice.
  4. Stack it up. Layer turkey, bacon, avocado, tomato, and lettuce on 4 slices, then top with the other slices.
  5. Cut and serve. Cut each sandwich in half and serve.
