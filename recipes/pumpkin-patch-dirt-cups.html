Thomas's Pumpkin Patch Dirt Cups

Chocolatey pudding cups topped with cookie-crumb dirt, gummy worms, and a candy pumpkin. Tastes like digging into a pumpkin patch.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
CookNo-bake
Serves6 cups
Nutritionabout 320 cal · 4g protein per cup

Crush the cookies in a zip-top bag with a rolling pin — fast and no mess.

What you need

  • 1 box (3.9 ounces) chocolate instant pudding mix (one small box)
  • 2 cups cold milk
  • 1 tub (8 ounces) whipped topping, thawed (like Cool Whip — one regular tub)
  • 20 chocolate sandwich cookies (like Oreos — about half a package)
  • 12 gummy worms
  • 6 candy pumpkins (the little orange ones)

Also grab: 6 small clear cups, a big bowl, a whisk, and a zip-top bag.

How to make it

  1. Make the pudding. Whisk the pudding mix and cold milk in a big bowl for 2 minutes until it thickens up.
  2. Fold in the fluff. Stir in the whole tub of whipped topping until it's smooth and light brown.
  3. Crush the cookies. Put the cookies in a zip-top bag and crush them with a rolling pin until they look like dirt.
  4. Fill the cups. Spoon the pudding mixture into 6 small cups, then sprinkle the cookie crumbs thick on top of each one.
  5. Decorate. Stick 2 gummy worms halfway into each cup and press a candy pumpkin on top. Chill 30 minutes and serve.
