Thomas's Veggie Egg Muffins

Eggs, cheese, and veggies baked in a muffin tin. Grab-and-go breakfast for busy mornings.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook20 minutes
Serves6 servings
Nutritionabout 180 cal · 12g protein per serving

Grease the tin well or line it — eggs love to stick.

What you need

  • 6 eggs
  • 1/4 cup milk (whole or 2%)
  • 1 bell pepper, diced small
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1/4 cup diced onion (optional)
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

Also grab: a muffin tin, a bowl, and a whisk.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 350 degrees.
  2. Whisk the eggs. In a bowl, whisk the eggs, milk, salt, and pepper.
  3. Add the veggies. Stir in the pepper, cheese, and onion if using.
  4. Fill the tin. Grease a muffin tin and fill each cup about 3/4 full.
  5. Bake. Bake 20 minutes till set and lightly golden.
  6. Cool and serve. Cool a few minutes, pop them out, and serve warm or cold.
