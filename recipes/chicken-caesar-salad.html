Thomas's Chicken Caesar Salad

Crisp lettuce, grilled chicken, croutons, and creamy Caesar dressing. A classic done right.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Cook10 minutes
Serves2 servings
Nutritionabout 480 cal · 38g protein per serving

Toss the dressing in right before serving so the lettuce stays crisp.

What you need

  • 2 chicken breasts (or 2 cups cooked chicken, sliced)
  • 1 head romaine lettuce, chopped
  • 1 cup croutons
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/2 cup Caesar dressing (store-bought)
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Also grab: a pan, a bowl, and tongs.

How to make it

  1. Cook the chicken. Season the chicken with salt and pepper and cook in a pan 5–6 minutes per side till done. Slice it. (Skip if using cooked chicken.)
  2. Build the salad. In a bowl, toss the lettuce, croutons, and Parmesan.
  3. Dress it. Pour in the dressing and toss till coated.
  4. Serve. Top with the sliced chicken and serve right away.
