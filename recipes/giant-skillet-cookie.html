Thomas's Giant Skillet Chocolate Chip Cookie

One big warm cookie with crispy edges and a gooey middle—serve it straight from the skillet with ice cream.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Bake20–25 minutes
TotalAbout 35 minutes
Serves8 people
Nutritionabout 450 cal · 5g protein per serving

Take it out while the center still looks a little soft for the best gooey middle.

What you need

  • ½ cup butter, melted
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • ¼ cup white sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1½ cups flour
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup chocolate chips
  • Vanilla ice cream, for serving

Also grab: a mixing bowl and a 10-inch oven-safe skillet.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Mix the sugars and butter. Stir the melted butter, brown sugar and white sugar together until smooth.
  3. Add the wet ingredients. Mix in the egg and vanilla.
  4. Add the dry ingredients. Stir in the flour, baking soda and salt just until combined.
  5. Add the chocolate. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  6. Fill the skillet. Press the dough evenly into the oven-safe skillet.
  7. Bake for 20–25 minutes. The edges should be golden and the center should be just barely set.
  8. Cool for 10 minutes, then serve warm with vanilla ice cream.

Don't overbake it

Pull it out when the center looks a little soft. It will finish cooking in the hot skillet.

Save the leftovers

Cover and keep at room temperature for up to 3 days.
