Thomas's Veggie Stir Fry with Rice

Crunchy veggies in a sweet-salty sauce over fluffy rice. One pan, one pot, dinner in about half an hour.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Cook20 minutes
Serves4 servings
Nutritionabout 340 cal · 8g protein per serving

Cut everything before you start cooking — stir fry moves fast.

What you need

  • 1 cup white rice (one regular coffee mug full, uncooked)
  • 2 cups water
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 cups broccoli florets (cut into small bite-size pieces)
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced (cut off the top, scoop out the seeds, then slice)
  • 2 carrots, sliced thin
  • 1 cup snap peas
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon maple syrup (or honey if you like it)
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground ginger (the dried spice, not fresh)
  • 1 tablespoon sesame seeds (optional — for sprinkling on top)

Also grab: a big pan or wok, a pot with a lid, and a cutting board.

How to make it

  1. Cook the rice first. Put the rice and water in a pot with a lid. Bring it to a boil, then turn the heat to low and put the lid on. Cook for 15 minutes, then turn off the heat and leave the lid on for 5 more minutes. Fluff it with a fork.
  2. Cut up the veggies. While the rice cooks, cut the broccoli into small pieces, slice the pepper and carrots, and pull the strings off the snap peas if they have any.
  3. Mix the sauce. Stir the soy sauce, maple syrup, garlic powder, and ginger together in a small bowl. That is your sauce — easy.
  4. Stir fry the veggies. Heat the oil in a big pan over high heat until it shimmers. Add the broccoli and carrots first and cook for 3 minutes, stirring a lot. Then add the pepper and snap peas and cook 2 more minutes. You want them bright and crunchy, not soft.
  5. Add the sauce. Pour the sauce over the veggies and stir for 1 minute until everything is shiny and coated.
  6. Serve it over rice. Spoon rice into bowls, pile the veggies on top, and sprinkle with sesame seeds if you have them.
