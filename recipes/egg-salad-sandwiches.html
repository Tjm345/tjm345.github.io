Thomas's Egg Salad Sandwiches

Creamy, tangy egg salad with a little crunch from celery, piled on soft bread with a shake of paprika. Simple and perfect.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Cook10 minutes
Serves4 sandwiches
Nutritionabout 380 cal · 14g protein per sandwich

Chill the egg salad at least 30 minutes so the flavors come together.

What you need

  • 8 large eggs
  • ½ cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon yellow mustard
  • 2 celery stalks, diced small
  • ¼ small red onion, diced small (about 2 tablespoons)
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • ¼ teaspoon paprika, plus more for the tops
  • 8 slices of soft sandwich bread
  • lettuce leaves (optional)

Also grab: a medium pot with a lid, a big bowl of ice water, a mixing bowl and a fork.

How to make it

  1. Boil the eggs.. Put the eggs in the pot in a single layer and cover with cold water by about an inch. Bring to a full rolling boil over high heat, then turn the heat off, put the lid on and let them sit 10 minutes.
  2. Cool them fast.. Scoop the eggs into the bowl of ice water with a spoon and let them sit 5 minutes. Cold eggs peel way easier.
  3. Peel and chop.. Tap each egg on the counter and roll it gently to crack the shell all over, then peel. Chop the eggs into small bite-size pieces — not too tiny, you want some texture.
  4. Mix the salad.. Put the chopped eggs in the mixing bowl. Add the mayonnaise, mustard, celery, red onion, salt, pepper and the ¼ teaspoon of paprika. Stir gently until everything is coated. Taste it — add a pinch more salt if it needs it.
  5. Chill it.. Cover the bowl and refrigerate at least 30 minutes. This rest makes a real difference in flavor.
  6. Build the sandwiches.. Spread a thick layer of egg salad on 4 slices of bread. Add lettuce if you like, shake a little paprika over the top, and close with the other 4 slices. Cut in half and serve.

How to serve it

Great with potato chips and a pickle. Also really good scooped onto crackers or stuffed into a pita.

Save the leftovers

Refrigerate for up to 3 days in a sealed container. Do not freeze — the mayo gets weird.
