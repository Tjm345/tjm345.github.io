Thomas's Crispy Fried Pickles with Ranch Dip

Tangy dill pickle chips in a crunchy golden coating, fried crisp and served with cool ranch. The appetizer that disappears first.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Cook15 minutes
Serves6
Nutritionabout 290 cal · 6g protein per serving

Pat the pickles really dry first — that is the secret to a coating that sticks.

What you need

  • 1 jar dill pickle chips, 24 ounces, drained
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt, plus more for sprinkling
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • 1½ cups panko breadcrumbs
  • 4 cups vegetable oil for frying (about)
  • ranch dressing for dipping

Also grab: three shallow bowls, a large heavy pot or deep skillet, a slotted spoon and paper towels.

How to make it

  1. Dry the pickles.. Spread the pickle chips on paper towels and pat them very dry on both sides. Wet pickles make the coating slide off.
  2. Set up your station.. Bowl 1: flour, paprika, garlic powder, salt and pepper stirred together. Bowl 2: beaten eggs. Bowl 3: panko.
  3. Bread them.. Coat each pickle chip in flour, then egg, then press into the panko so it sticks. Set them on a plate as you go.
  4. Heat the oil.. Pour the oil into the pot to about 2 inches deep and heat over medium-high until a panko crumb sizzles right away — about 350°F.
  5. Fry in batches.. Fry a small handful at a time for 2–3 minutes, turning once, until deep golden. Do not crowd the pot.
  6. Drain and salt.. Scoop out with the slotted spoon onto paper towels and sprinkle with a little salt right away. Serve hot with ranch.

How to serve it

Eat them hot — they are at their crispiest in the first 10 minutes.

Save the leftovers

Honestly, these do not keep well. Make only what you will eat right away.
