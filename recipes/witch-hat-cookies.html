Thomas's Witch Hat Cookies

Striped chocolate cookies topped with a chocolate kiss — instant witch hats, no baking at all.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep20 minutes
CookNo-bake
Serves24 cookies
Nutritionabout 130 cal · 1g protein per cookie

A dab of melted chocolate works like glue — don't skip it or the hats slide off.

What you need

  • 24 fudge-striped shortbread cookies (one package — like Keebler Fudge Stripes)
  • 24 chocolate kiss candies, unwrapped
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips (for glue)
  • Orange sprinkles (optional)

Also grab: a baking sheet, parchment paper, and a microwave-safe bowl.

How to make it

  1. Set up. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper and lay out the cookies stripe-side down.
  2. Melt the glue. Microwave the chocolate chips in 20-second bursts, stirring each time, until smooth.
  3. Build the hats. Dab a little melted chocolate in the center of each cookie and press a chocolate kiss on top.
  4. Decorate. While the chocolate is still wet, sprinkle orange sprinkles around the base of each hat if you like.
  5. Let them set. Leave them on the counter about 30 minutes until the chocolate hardens, then they're ready.
