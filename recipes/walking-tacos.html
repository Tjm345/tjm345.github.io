Thomas's Walking Tacos

Taco meat and all the fixings served right in individual chip bags. No plates, no mess, all fun.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Cook15 minutes
Serves8 bags
Nutritionabout 460 cal · 20g protein per bag

Crush the chips in the bag a little first — it makes scooping way easier.

What you need

  • 1 pound ground beef (one store package)
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 8 individual bags of corn chips (like Fritos or Doritos snack bags)
  • 2 cups shredded lettuce
  • 1 cup diced tomatoes
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • Sour cream and salsa (for topping)

Also grab: a skillet and a spoon.

How to make it

  1. Cook the beef. Brown the ground beef in a skillet, drain the fat, and stir in the taco seasoning with a splash of water.
  2. Open the bags. Cut the top off each chip bag and crush the chips lightly inside.
  3. Build them. Spoon taco beef into each bag, then let everyone add lettuce, tomatoes, cheese, sour cream, and salsa.
  4. Eat. Grab a fork and eat straight out of the bag.
