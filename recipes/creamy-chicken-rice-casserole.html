Thomas's Creamy Chicken & Rice Casserole

Tender chicken and rice baked in a creamy cheesy sauce. One dish, zero fuss, total comfort food.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Cook40 minutes
Serves6 people
Nutritionabout 650 cal · 40g protein per serving

Keep the dish covered for the first 35 minutes so the rice can steam until tender.

What you need

  • 1½ pounds chicken breasts (about 3), cut into bite-size pieces
  • 1½ cups uncooked long-grain white rice
  • 1 can cream of chicken soup (10.5 ounces)
  • 1½ cups chicken broth
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese, divided
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon pepper
  • 1 cup frozen peas (optional)

Also grab: a big mixing bowl, a 9 × 13-inch baking dish and foil.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F.
  2. Mix the creamy base. In a big bowl, stir together the rice, soup, broth, sour cream, half the cheese, garlic powder, onion powder, salt and pepper.
  3. Add the chicken. Fold in the chicken pieces and the peas if you're using them.
  4. Fill the dish. Pour everything into a greased 9 × 13-inch baking dish and cover it tight with foil.
  5. Bake for 40 minutes. After 35 minutes, uncover it, sprinkle the rest of the cheese on top and bake for 5 more minutes until bubbly. The chicken should reach 165°F in the center.
  6. Let it sit for 5 minutes before scooping. It thickens up as it rests.
