Thomas's Berry Yogurt Parfait

Layers of yogurt, berries, and crunchy granola. A fresh, easy breakfast that looks fancy.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
CookNo cooking
Serves2 servings
Nutritionabout 240 cal · 10g protein per serving

Layer it right before eating so the granola stays crunchy.

What you need

  • 2 cups yogurt (vanilla or plain)
  • 1 cup mixed berries (fresh or thawed)
  • 1 cup granola
  • 1 tablespoon honey (optional)

Also grab: 2 glasses and a spoon.

How to make it

  1. Layer it up. In each glass, layer yogurt, berries, and granola. Repeat once.
  2. Top it off. Drizzle with honey if you like.
  3. Serve. Serve right away so the granola stays crunchy.
