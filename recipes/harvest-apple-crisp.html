Thomas's Harvest Apple Crisp

Warm cinnamon apples under a buttery oat crumble. The whole house smells like fall while it bakes.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Cook40 minutes
Serves8 people
Nutritionabout 290 cal · 3g protein per serving

Any apple works, but mixing two kinds gives the best flavor.

What you need

  • 6 apples, peeled and sliced thin
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1 cup old-fashioned oats
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar, packed
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup butter, melted (one stick)
  • Vanilla ice cream (for serving — optional but worth it)

Also grab: a 9x13 baking dish, two bowls, and a fork or pastry cutter.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 350°F. Grease a 9x13 baking dish with a little butter.
  2. Fill it. Toss the apple slices with the sugar and cinnamon right in the dish and spread them out.
  3. Make the crumble. In a bowl, mix the oats, brown sugar, and flour, then stir in the melted butter until it looks like wet sand.
  4. Top and bake. Sprinkle the crumble all over the apples. Bake 35–40 minutes until golden and bubbly around the edges.
  5. Serve warm. Scoop it into bowls while it's warm — add ice cream if you're feeling it.
