Thomas's Beef Enchilada Casserole

Layers of tortillas, seasoned beef, sauce, and melted cheese, baked bubbly in one dish. All the enchilada taste without the rolling.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Cook30 minutes
Serves6 servings
Nutritionabout 460 cal · 26g protein per serving

Let it sit 5 minutes after baking so the layers hold when you scoop.

What you need

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 packet taco seasoning (one standard packet)
  • 2 cups enchilada sauce (one can or jar)
  • 8 flour tortillas (the taco-size kind, torn in half)
  • 3 cups shredded Mexican cheese blend (one 12-ounce bag)
  • 1 can black beans, drained (optional)
  • Sour cream for serving (optional)

Also grab: a big pan, a baking dish, and a spoon.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 375 degrees.
  2. Cook the beef. In a big pan, cook the beef and onion till the beef is browned, breaking it up. Drain the fat, then stir in the taco seasoning and beans if using.
  3. Layer it up. Spread a little sauce in the baking dish, then layer torn tortillas, beef, sauce, and cheese. Repeat till everything is used, ending with cheese on top.
  4. Bake. Cover with foil and bake 20 minutes, then uncover and bake 10 more minutes till bubbly and golden.
  5. Rest and serve. Let it sit 5 minutes, then scoop out servings with sour cream if you like.
