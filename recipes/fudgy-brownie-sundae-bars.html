Thomas's Fudgy Brownie Sundae Bars

Thick fudgy brownie bars loaded with chocolate chips, served warm under ice cream and hot fudge. Dessert doesn't get better.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook25 minutes
Serves12 people
Nutritionabout 400 cal · 5g protein per bar (before toppings)

A toothpick with moist crumbs means the brownies are fudgy instead of overbaked.

What you need

  • 1 box fudge brownie mix (18 ounces), plus the eggs and oil it calls for
  • ½ cup chocolate chips
  • Vanilla ice cream, for serving
  • Hot fudge sauce, warmed
  • Whipped cream and sprinkles (optional but encouraged)

Also grab: a mixing bowl, a spoon, a 9 × 13-inch pan and parchment paper.

How to make them

  1. Heat the oven. Use the temperature on the brownie box, usually 350°F. Line a 9 × 13-inch pan with parchment or grease it.
  2. Mix the brownies using the directions on the box, then fold in the chocolate chips.
  3. Bake them. Spread the batter in the pan and bake for about 22–25 minutes, or the time on the box, until a toothpick comes out with moist crumbs.
  4. Cool for 15 minutes, then cut into 12 bars.
  5. Build the sundaes. Serve the bars warm with a scoop of ice cream, hot fudge drizzled over the top and whipped cream if you're feeling it.
