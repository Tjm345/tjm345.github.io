Thomas's Parmesan Roasted Green Beans

Crispy roasted green beans with salty Parmesan. An easy side that goes with almost anything.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook20 minutes
Serves4 servings
Nutritionabout 120 cal · 5g protein per serving

Spread them in one layer — crowded beans steam instead of crisping.

What you need

  • 1 pound green beans (one bag, ends trimmed)
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder

Also grab: a baking sheet and a bowl.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 425 degrees.
  2. Toss the beans. In a bowl, toss the green beans with olive oil, salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
  3. Spread them out. Spread the beans on a baking sheet in one layer.
  4. Roast. Roast 15 minutes, then sprinkle the Parmesan over and roast 5 more minutes till golden and crispy at the edges.
  5. Serve. Serve warm.
