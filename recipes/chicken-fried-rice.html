Thomas's Chicken Fried Rice

Day-old rice, chicken, and veggies tossed in soy sauce. Better than takeout, and fast.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook15 minutes
Serves4 servings
Nutritionabout 460 cal · 32g protein per serving

Cold rice fries up way better than fresh hot rice — it doesn't get mushy.

What you need

  • 1 pound chicken breast, cut into small pieces
  • 3 cups cold cooked rice (day-old is best)
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (frozen peas and carrots)
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced (chopped very small)
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • Green onions for topping, optional

Also grab: a big pan or wok, and a spoon.

How to make it

  1. Cook the chicken. Heat the oil in a big pan, cook the chicken till done, then take it out.
  2. Scramble the eggs. In the same pan, scramble the eggs, then take them out too.
  3. Fry the rice. Add a little more oil, then the rice, vegetables, and garlic. Stir-fry 5 minutes.
  4. Bring it together. Put the chicken and eggs back, pour in the soy sauce, and toss everything together.
  5. Serve. Top with green onions and serve warm.
