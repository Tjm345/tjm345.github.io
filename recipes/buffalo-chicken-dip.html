Thomas's Buffalo Chicken Dip

Creamy, cheesy, spicy buffalo chicken dip baked until bubbly. Scoop it up with tortilla chips — warning: it goes fast.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Bake20 minutes
Serves10 servings
Nutritionabout 280 cal · 14g protein per serving

Stir halfway through baking so the cheese melts evenly.

What you need

  • 2 cups shredded cooked chicken (one store rotisserie chicken)
  • 2 packages cream cheese, 8 ounces each, softened
  • ½ cup buffalo hot sauce
  • ½ cup ranch dressing
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese (one 8-ounce bag), divided
  • ¼ cup crumbled blue cheese (optional)
  • tortilla chips for serving

Also grab: a mixing bowl, a spoon and an 8x8 baking dish or pie plate.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F.. Grease your baking dish lightly.
  2. Soften the cream cheese.. Let it sit on the counter 20 minutes first, or microwave it 20 seconds. Soft cream cheese mixes way easier.
  3. Mix it all up.. Stir together the cream cheese, hot sauce and ranch until smooth. Fold in the chicken and 1½ cups of the cheddar.
  4. Spread and top.. Spread the mix in the dish and sprinkle the rest of the cheddar and the blue cheese on top if using.
  5. Bake 20 minutes.. Stir once halfway through. It is done when hot, bubbly and golden on top.
  6. Serve hot.. Set it out with a big bowl of tortilla chips and let everyone dig in.

How to serve it

Tortilla chips, celery sticks and carrot sticks for dipping.

Save the leftovers

Refrigerate up to 4 days. Reheat in the microwave or oven until hot and bubbly.
