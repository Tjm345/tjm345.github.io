Recipe 129 · Dessert

Thomas's Lemon Bar Squares

Buttery crust with a tangy lemon top, dusted with sugar. Bright, sweet, and simple.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Cook35 minutes
Serves16 squares
Nutritionabout 200 cal · 2g protein per square

Let them cool all the way before cutting, or the lemon top won't set.

What you need

  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 1/2 cup powdered sugar (for the crust, plus more for dusting)
  • 2 cups flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups sugar
  • 1/2 cup lemon juice (fresh or bottled)
  • 1 teaspoon lemon zest (optional, the yellow part of the peel)

Also grab: a bowl, a pan (9x13), and a whisk.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 350 degrees.
  2. Make the crust. Mix the butter, 1/2 cup powdered sugar, and flour till crumbly, then press into the pan.
  3. Bake the crust. Bake 15 minutes till lightly golden.
  4. Make the lemon top. Whisk the eggs, sugar, lemon juice, and zest.
  5. Bake again. Pour over the hot crust and bake 20 more minutes till set.
  6. Cool and cut. Cool all the way, dust with powdered sugar, and cut into squares.
