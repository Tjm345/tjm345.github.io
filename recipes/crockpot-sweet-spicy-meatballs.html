Thomas's Crockpot Sweet & Spicy Meatballs

Frozen meatballs simmered in a sweet-spicy sauce right in the crockpot. Three ingredients, zero work.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep5 minutes
Cook3 hours
Serves10 people
Nutritionabout 280 cal · 12g protein per serving

Stir once halfway through so the sauce coats every meatball.

What you need

  • 1 bag frozen homestyle meatballs (one 32-ounce bag — don't thaw them)
  • 1 jar grape jelly (one 18-ounce jar)
  • 1 bottle chili sauce (one 12-ounce bottle — like Heinz, found by the ketchup)

Also grab: a crockpot and a spoon.

How to make it

  1. Dump it in. Put the frozen meatballs in the crockpot. Spoon the grape jelly and chili sauce right on top.
  2. Stir. Stir it all together as best you can — it'll melt together as it cooks.
  3. Cook low and slow. Cover and cook on low 3 hours, stirring once halfway through.
  4. Serve. Turn the crockpot to warm and serve the meatballs straight from the pot with toothpicks.
