Recipe 109 · Breakfast

Thomas's Blueberry Lemon Pancakes

Fluffy pancakes packed with blueberries and a fresh lemon taste, with syrup dripping down the stack.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook15 minutes
Serves4 servings
Nutritionabout 350 cal · 8g protein per serving

Zest the lemon before you cut it — it's way easier that way.

What you need

  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups milk (whole or 2%)
  • 2 tablespoons butter, melted (plus more for the pan)
  • 1 lemon (for zest and juice — zest first, then squeeze)
  • 1 1/2 cups blueberries (one small box)
  • Syrup for serving

Also grab: a big bowl, a whisk, and a pan or griddle.

How to make it

  1. Mix the dry stuff. In a big bowl, mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
  2. Mix the wet stuff. In another bowl, whisk the eggs, milk, melted butter, lemon zest, and a squeeze of lemon juice.
  3. Put them together. Pour the wet mix into the dry mix and stir just till mixed — a few lumps are fine.
  4. Add the blueberries. Fold the blueberries in gently so they don't all burst.
  5. Cook the pancakes. Melt a little butter in the pan on medium heat, scoop in batter, and cook till bubbles show on top, then flip and cook the other side till golden.
  6. Serve. Stack them up and pour syrup over the top.
