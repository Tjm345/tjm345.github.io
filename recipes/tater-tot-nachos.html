Thomas's Tater Tot Nachos (Totchos)

Nachos, but on crispy tater tots instead of chips. Crunchy, cheesy, and gone in about five minutes.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook25 minutes
Serves6 people
Nutritionabout 480 cal · 16g protein per serving

Bake the tots extra crispy — they hold up way better under the toppings.

What you need

  • 1 bag frozen tater tots (one 32-ounce bag)
  • 1 pound ground beef (one store package)
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 2 cups shredded Mexican-blend cheese (one 8-ounce bag)
  • Sour cream, salsa, and sliced jalapeños (for the top)

Also grab: a big baking sheet and parchment paper.

How to make it

  1. Crisp the tots. Spread the tater tots on a lined baking sheet and bake per the bag until deep golden and crispy.
  2. Cook the beef. While they bake, brown the ground beef in a skillet, drain the fat, and stir in the taco seasoning with a splash of water.
  3. Pile it on. Spoon the taco beef over the hot tots and cover everything with shredded cheese.
  4. Melt. Bake 5 more minutes until the cheese is melted.
  5. Top it. Dollop with sour cream, spoon on salsa, and add jalapeños if you like heat. Serve right away.
