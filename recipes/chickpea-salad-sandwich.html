Thomas's Chickpea Salad Sandwich

Mashed chickpeas, crunchy celery, and a creamy lemon dressing on soft bread. Like a tuna sandwich, but no fish — and no cooking at all.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
Cookno cooking needed
Serves4 sandwiches
Nutritionabout 380 cal · 12g protein per sandwich

Mash the chickpeas chunky — a few whole ones left in is perfect.

What you need

  • 1 can chickpeas (15 ounces — one regular store can, drained and rinsed)
  • 3 tablespoons vegan mayonnaise (the plant-based kind, in the same aisle as regular mayo)
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice (about half a lemon, squeezed)
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • 2 stalks celery, diced small
  • ¼ red onion, diced small
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • 8 slices whole wheat bread
  • 4 leaves lettuce

Also grab: a can opener, a big bowl, a fork, and a knife.

How to make it

  1. Drain the chickpeas. Open the can, pour the chickpeas into a strainer, and rinse them under cold water. Shake off the extra water.
  2. Mash them. Put the chickpeas in a big bowl and mash them with a fork. You want them mostly squished but still chunky — leave a few whole ones in there.
  3. Mix in the creamy stuff. Stir in the vegan mayonnaise, lemon juice, and Dijon mustard until it looks like a thick salad.
  4. Add the crunch. Stir in the celery, red onion, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Taste it — want more zing? Add a little more lemon juice.
  5. Build your sandwiches. Lay out 4 slices of bread. Put a lettuce leaf on each one, spoon the chickpea salad on top, and put the other 4 slices on top.
  6. Cut and serve. Cut each sandwich in half. They are ready to eat — no cooking needed.
