Thomas's Strawberry Icebox Cake

Layers of wafers, cream, and strawberries that soften into cake in the fridge. No oven needed.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep20 minutes
Cook4 hours chilling
Serves8 servings
Nutritionabout 320 cal · 5g protein per serving

Chill it overnight if you can — the longer it sits, the more cake-like it gets.

What you need

  • 2 cups heavy cream (or 1 big tub whipped topping)
  • 1/4 cup powdered sugar (if whipping your own cream)
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 2 boxes graham crackers
  • 2 cups strawberries, sliced

Also grab: a dish (9x13), a bowl, and a whisk.

How to make it

  1. Whip the cream. If using heavy cream, whip it with the powdered sugar and vanilla till soft peaks. If using whipped topping, skip this.
  2. Layer it. In the dish, layer graham crackers, cream, and strawberries. Repeat till you run out, ending with cream.
  3. Chill. Cover and chill at least 4 hours, or overnight.
  4. Serve. Slice and serve cold, topped with extra strawberries.
