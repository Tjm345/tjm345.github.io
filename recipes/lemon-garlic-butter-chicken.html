Thomas's Lemon Garlic Butter Chicken

Golden seared chicken in a bright lemon garlic butter sauce. Simple, fast, and good with rice or potatoes.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook20 minutes
Serves4 servings
Nutritionabout 380 cal · 36g protein per serving

Let the chicken rest a couple minutes before slicing — it stays juicier.

What you need

  • 4 chicken breasts (boneless, skinless)
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons butter (plus 2 more for the sauce)
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced (or 1 teaspoon garlic powder)
  • 1 lemon (juice and zest — zest first)
  • 1/2 cup chicken broth (or water)
  • 1 tablespoon parsley, chopped (optional, for the top)

Also grab: a big pan, a spatula or tongs, and a knife.

How to make it

  1. Season the chicken. Sprinkle salt and pepper on both sides of the chicken breasts.
  2. Sear the chicken. Melt 2 tablespoons butter in a big pan on medium-high heat, add the chicken, and cook about 6 minutes per side till golden and cooked through. Set aside.
  3. Make the sauce. In the same pan, melt the rest of the butter, add the garlic, and cook 1 minute till it smells good.
  4. Add lemon and broth. Pour in the lemon juice, zest, and broth, and stir, scraping up the brown bits.
  5. Finish and serve. Put the chicken back in the pan, spoon sauce over it, sprinkle parsley if using, and serve.
