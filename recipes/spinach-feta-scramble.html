Thomas's Spinach Feta Scramble

Fluffy eggs with spinach and salty feta. A fast, filling breakfast.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep5 minutes
Cook10 minutes
Serves2 servings
Nutritionabout 260 cal · 18g protein per serving

Pull the eggs off the heat just before they look done — they keep cooking in the pan.

What you need

  • 4 eggs
  • 1 cup fresh spinach
  • 1/4 cup crumbled feta cheese
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • Toast for serving, optional

Also grab: a pan and a spatula.

How to make it

  1. Whisk the eggs. In a bowl, whisk the eggs, salt, and pepper.
  2. Wilt the spinach. Melt the butter in a pan, add the spinach, and cook 1 minute till wilted.
  3. Scramble. Pour in the eggs and stir gently till just set.
  4. Add feta. Take off the heat, stir in the feta, and serve warm, with toast if you like.
