Thomas's Denver Omelet Skillet

Diced ham, sweet bell peppers and onions folded into one big fluffy omelet. One skillet feeds the whole table.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep10 minutes
Cook15 minutes
Serves4 servings
Nutritionabout 390 cal · 26g protein per serving

Keep the heat at medium — high heat browns the eggs before the middle sets.

What you need

  • 10 large eggs
  • 1 cup diced ham (one 8-ounce package)
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese (half an 8-ounce bag)
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper

Also grab: a large oven-safe skillet, a mixing bowl, a whisk and a spatula.

How to make it

  1. Cook the ham and veggies.. Melt 1 tablespoon of butter in the skillet over medium heat. Cook the ham, bell pepper and onion for 5–6 minutes, stirring, until the veggies are soft. Scoop onto a plate.
  2. Whisk the eggs.. Crack all 10 eggs into the bowl. Add the salt and pepper and whisk until fully mixed and a little frothy.
  3. Start the omelet.. Melt the remaining butter in the same skillet over medium heat. Pour in the eggs and let them sit for 1 minute until the edges start to set.
  4. Add the filling.. Spread the ham and veggie mixture over one half of the eggs and sprinkle the cheese over everything.
  5. Fold it over.. When the eggs are mostly set but still a little wet on top (about 4–5 minutes), slide the spatula under the empty half and fold it over the filled half.
  6. Finish and serve.. Cook 1–2 more minutes until the cheese melts, then slide the whole omelet onto a plate and cut into 4 wedges.

How to serve it

Hot sauce and a side of toast. This is a full breakfast all by itself.

Save the leftovers

Refrigerate for up to 2 days. Reheat gently in a skillet over low heat.
