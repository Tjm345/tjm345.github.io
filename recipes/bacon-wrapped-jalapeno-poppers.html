Thomas's Bacon-Wrapped Jalapeño Poppers

Jalapeños stuffed with cream cheese, wrapped in bacon, and baked until crispy. The ultimate game day bite.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep20 minutes
Cook25 minutes
Serves12 poppers
Nutritionabout 120 cal · 5g protein per popper

Cut the bacon slices in half first — a full slice is too much and won't get crispy.

What you need

  • 12 jalapeños, halved and seeded
  • 1 block cream cheese, softened (8 ounces)
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 12 slices bacon, cut in half

Also grab: a baking sheet, parchment paper, and toothpicks.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 400 degrees and line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Make the filling. Stir the cream cheese and cheddar together in a bowl until mixed.
  3. Stuff them. Spoon the cheese mix into each jalapeño half, filling them full.
  4. Wrap in bacon. Wrap a half-slice of bacon around each one and poke a toothpick through to hold it.
  5. Bake. Bake 20 to 25 minutes until the bacon is crispy. Cool 5 minutes before eating — the filling is lava-hot.
