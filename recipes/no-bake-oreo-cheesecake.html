Thomas's No-Bake Oreo Cheesecake

Cookies-and-cream filling on a buttery Oreo crust—rich, creamy and no oven needed.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep20 minutes
Chill4 hours
TotalAbout 4½ hours
Serves10–12 people
Nutritionabout 550 cal · 7g protein per slice

Make it the night before so it is fully chilled and cuts into clean slices.

What you need

  • For the crust
  • 24 Oreo cookies
  • 5 tablespoons butter, melted
  • For the filling
  • 16 ounces cream cheese, softened (two blocks)
  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1½ cups whipped cream or whipped topping
  • 10 extra Oreos, crushed

How to make it

  1. Make the crumbs. Crush 24 Oreos into fine crumbs, then mix them with the melted butter.
  2. Build the crust. Press the crumbs firmly into the bottom and up the sides of a 9-inch pie dish. Chill for 30 minutes.
  3. Mix the filling. Beat the softened cream cheese, powdered sugar and vanilla until smooth.
  4. Add the cookies and cream. Gently fold in the whipped cream and crushed Oreos.
  5. Fill the crust. Spread the mixture over the chilled crust and smooth the top.
  6. Chill for at least 4 hours or leave it in the fridge overnight.

Plan ahead

Make it the night before. The slices come out cleanest when the cheesecake is fully chilled.

Save the leftovers

Cover and refrigerate for up to 4 days.
