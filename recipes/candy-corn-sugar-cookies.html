Thomas's Candy Corn Sugar Cookies

Soft triangle sugar cookies iced in candy corn stripes. They look like they took all day — they didn't.

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep30 minutes
Cook10 minutes
Serves24 cookies
Nutritionabout 150 cal · 2g protein per cookie

A zip-top bag with the corner snipped works great as a piping bag.

What you need

  • 2 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup butter, softened (two sticks, room temp)
  • 1 1/2 cups sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 2 cups powdered sugar
  • 3 tablespoons milk
  • Orange and yellow food coloring

Also grab: a baking sheet, parchment paper, bowls, and zip-top bags for icing.

How to make it

  1. Heat the oven. Set it to 375°F. Line baking sheets with parchment.
  2. Make the dough. Whisk the flour, baking soda, and salt. Beat the butter and sugar fluffy, beat in the egg and vanilla, then stir in the dry stuff.
  3. Shape triangles. Roll dough into 1-inch balls, then pinch one end to make a triangle-ish candy corn shape. Flatten slightly.
  4. Bake. Bake 8–10 minutes until just set — don't brown them. Cool completely.
  5. Ice them. Mix powdered sugar and milk into a thick icing. Split into 3 bowls: leave one white, color one orange, one yellow. Ice each cookie in 3 stripes — white tip, orange middle, yellow base. Let set 1 hour.
