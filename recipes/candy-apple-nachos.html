Thomas's Candy Apple Nachos

Thin apple slices loaded up like nachos — caramel, chocolate, peanuts, and candy. Made for sharing (or not).

Jump to recipe ↓
Prep15 minutes
CookNo-bake
Serves6 people
Nutritionabout 240 cal · 2g protein per serving

Slice the apples thin so they scoop up the toppings like chips.

What you need

  • 4 crisp apples, sliced thin
  • 1/2 cup caramel sauce (the squeezy ice-cream-topping kind)
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips, melted
  • 1/4 cup chopped peanuts
  • 1/4 cup mini chocolate candies (like mini M&M's)
  • 2 tablespoons sprinkles (Halloween colors if you have them)

Also grab: a big plate or platter and a microwave-safe bowl.

How to make it

  1. Spread the apples. Arrange the apple slices in one layer on a big plate, overlapping a little like nacho chips.
  2. Drizzle everything. Drizzle the caramel sauce and melted chocolate all over the slices.
  3. Load the toppings. Sprinkle the peanuts, mini chocolate candies, and sprinkles over the top.
  4. Serve right away. Hand out napkins — these are gloriously messy.
