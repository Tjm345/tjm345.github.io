Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Hi, I'm Thomas. This page explains in plain words what happens with your information when you use my website, thomassrecipes.com, and my Thomas's Kitchen app. The short version: I don't collect your personal information.

The app just shows the website

The Thomas's Kitchen app simply opens my website inside the app. It's the same recipes and the same pages you would see in a web browser.

No accounts

There is no sign-up and no login on my website or in the app. I don't ask for your name, your email, or any other personal information just for looking at recipes.

The app collects nothing

The app itself does not collect, store, or send me any information about you or your phone.

Counting visitors

My website uses Google Analytics, which uses cookies to count how many people visit and which pages and recipes are popular. That helps me know what people like to cook. It does not tell me who you are.

Links to other companies

My site has links to other places, like Gumroad, Amazon, and Shopify for my cookbooks and shop, and YouTube, TikTok, and Discord for my videos and chat. When you tap one of those links, you leave my site, and those companies have their own privacy policies.

The newsletter

If you sign up for my email newsletter, the signup is handled by Buttondown. The only thing they get is the email address you type in, so they can send you the newsletter. You can unsubscribe any time.

Questions

If you have a question about this policy, you can email me at thomasmoulton6475788@gmail.com.
